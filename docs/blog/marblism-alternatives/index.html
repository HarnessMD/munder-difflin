Marblism Alternatives: 5 Tools Like Marblism for AI Employees (Nov 2026)

Marblism sells seven named AI employees to businesses. Five alternatives, from Sintra to Claude Cowork, with prices checked 12 Nov 2026.

MD Chaitanya Giri · Nov 12, 2026 · 5 min read Comparisons

Our view: Sintra is the closest Marblism alternative, because it also sells a team of named AI employees to small business owners on one plan. Marblism, a Y Combinator Winter 2024 company, gives you seven of them, from an inbox assistant to a phone receptionist. Checked 12 Nov 2026.

We make Munder Difflin, which is free and open source: a desktop app that runs a team of AI agents for all kinds of knowledge work, coding included, on your own computer. The wider field is in our pinned Herdr alternatives guide and the Comparisons hub.

What is Marblism? #

Marblism sells AI employees to businesses, each with a name and one job. Its Y Combinator page, read 12 Nov 2026, calls them “AI Employees who love overtime” and lists the company in the Winter 2024 batch. The pricing page names the cast: “Your AI team can manage your inbox (Eva), run your social media (Sonny), generate leads (Stan), write SEO content (Penny), answer the phone (Rachel), build your website (Walter), and review contracts (Linda). All 7 AI Employees are included with every plan.”

How does Marblism work? #

You use it on the web and in its mobile apps, and the employees sign in to your tools. The home page says “Your AI employees log into your inbox, calendar, socials and website, and work where you work.” Its What’s new page added a web browser on 12 Oct 2026 and Rooms on 4 Nov 2026, where several employees and your teammates “work on the same project together”.

How much does Marblism cost? #

One plan has every employee, at $24 a month on the yearly option, per the pricing page read 12 Nov 2026; the monthly toggle showed $44 a month that day. Every plan “starts with 50 monthly hours of AI work that reset each month”, and when the hours run out you buy top ups. The table also rates “Office drama” and “Sick days” as “none”, which no paper company in Scranton could promise.

Why look for a Marblism alternative? #

Our view: for the hours, the yearly billing, or a plan you already pay for. Advanced work draws on 50 hours a month, the $24 price is billed yearly per a note on the pricing page read 12 Nov 2026, and paid Claude plans and xAI’s Premium plan already include an agent. Marblism’s own AI Employee Index, on the same page, grades nine products on 27 jobs and ranks Lindy, Sintra and Grok Bot behind itself. This list is for people comparing tools like Marblism before they pick.

1. Sintra: the closest match to Marblism #

Sintra sells 12 named AI employees, the nearest match to Marblism’s model. Its home page, read 12 Nov 2026, lists helpers such as Soshie for social media, Seomi for SEO and Vizzy as a virtual assistant, “Available 24/7 on web and mobile”. Its pricing page showed sale prices of $15.60 a month yearly and $48.50 monthly on 12 Nov 2026. It says it “won’t work” if you want “A power tool with AI coding ability”. It suits solo owners who want a named cast.

2. Munder Difflin: free, open source, your own computer #

Munder Difflin is ours. Its README, read 12 Nov 2026, says “Import a ready made role from the Agent Gallery if you would rather not start from scratch.” and offers “Download 0.5.6 for macOS, Windows or Linux”. Work arrives from Slack or a webhook, and agents gain tools through MCP servers, skills and plugins. Agents run on your computer, or in its Cloud Office, always on, with your computer off; a mobile app is coming soon. Our view: it suits research, writing, ops and code. Setup is in how to install and use Munder Difflin.

3. Lindy: one assistant that also takes calls #

Lindy is one AI employee that covers many roles. Its home page, read 12 Nov 2026, says “I work in Slack, email, text and your calendar, with 3,000+ integrations + MCP”. Its changelog added a phone number on 14 Oct 2026: “It calls businesses for you and answers as your receptionist.” On 12 Nov 2026 the pricing page showed paid use from $29.99 per user a month on some loads and $49.99 on others, after $50 in free credits. It suits teams that work in Slack.

4. Grok Bot: several Bots with set jobs #

Grok Bot is xAI’s set of AI teammates. The Grok Bot page, read 12 Nov 2026, says “Every Bot has its own computer” and “Give each Bot its own job, and add another when the work grows.” Version 1.0, per xAI’s launch post of 2 Nov 2026, works in Slack, Microsoft Teams and video calls. Premium was $30 a month billed monthly on 12 Nov 2026. It suits teams already on Premium or higher; see Grok Bot alternatives.

5. Claude Cowork: if you already pay for Claude #

Cowork hands Claude longer jobs. Anthropic’s help page, read 12 Nov 2026, says it “brings Claude Code’s agentic capabilities to knowledge work beyond coding” and “runs your sessions remotely in the cloud”, on paid plans only. Claude’s pricing page listed Pro at $20 a month billed monthly on 12 Nov 2026. It suits people who already pay for Claude; see Claude Cowork alternatives.

How to choose #

Match the tool to how you already work. Our view:

  • Sintra for a named cast on one plan, like Marblism.
  • Munder Difflin for a free, open source team of agents for research, writing, ops and code.
  • Lindy for one assistant across Slack, email and the phone.
  • Grok Bot if you already pay for xAI’s Premium plan or higher.
  • Claude Cowork if you already pay for Claude.
  • Stay on Marblism for a receptionist, a website builder and contract review in one plan.
