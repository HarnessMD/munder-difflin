Gemini 4 Argon: Google's Flagship Model Shares a Name With a Gas That Barely Reacts

Gemini 4 Argon went on general release on 13 Oct 2026: $2 input and $10 output per 1M tokens through 30 Nov, a 1M token window, and where you can use it.

What is Gemini 4 Argon? #

Gemini 4 Argon is Google’s newest top model, built for long jobs in coding, office work and security. Google’s developer post of 13 October 2026 calls it “our frontier model for complex workflows across real-world software engineering, enterprise knowledge work, and cybersecurity defense”.

Google’s posts do not say why it is called Argon. The element is a noble gas that reacts with almost nothing, which is not the sales pitch here.

The rows come from Google’s model card and models page, read on 13 Oct 2026.

Gemini 4 Argon
API model ID gemini-4-argon
Input Text, images, audio and video
Output Text
Context window Up to 1M tokens, input and output combined
Knowledge cutoff March 2026

It is the first of a set. Google’s launch post says “We’re looking forward to bringing you the rest of the Gemini 4 family”, with no names and no dates. The Hacker News thread had more than 400 points on 13 Oct 2026.

When was Gemini 4 Argon released? #

Gemini 4 Argon went on general release on 13 October 2026. Google’s launch post, dated Oct 13, 2026, says the model “is now globally available today for developers and enterprises”.

It was not a surprise. The Verge reported on 30 September 2026 that Google had revealed the model and was holding it for “trusted cyber defenders” at first. The curtain opened in two pulls.

Where can you use Gemini 4 Argon? #

You can use it through the paid Gemini API, in Google AI Studio and Antigravity, and in the Gemini app, depending on what you pay for. The rows are our summary of Google’s launch post and its Antigravity post, both dated 13 Oct 2026.

Where Who gets it
Gemini API Paid tier
Google AI Studio Paid tier, or Google AI Ultra subscribers
Android Studio Paid tier of the Gemini API
Antigravity desktop app and CLI Google AI Ultra subscribers
Gemini app and AI Mode in Search Google AI Ultra and Pro subscribers

The free door is shut. Google’s pricing page says Gemini 4 Argon “is not available on the Free tier or on the Google AI Pro plan”. That note sits on the API pricing page; the launch post still lists Pro subscribers for the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search, and Google’s plans page says it is “rolling out to Google AI Pro subscribers in select countries”. For businesses, the Antigravity post lists the API in Gemini platform and Antigravity “on eligible Gemini Enterprise subscriptions”.

What does Gemini 4 Argon cost? #

Gemini 4 Argon costs $2.00 input and $10.00 output per 1M tokens through 30 November 2026, then $4.00 and $20.00 from 1 December 2026, on Google’s pricing page as read on 13 Oct 2026. The output price includes thinking tokens.

Paid tier, per 1M tokens Through 30 Nov 2026 From 1 Dec 2026
Input $2.00 $4.00
Output $10.00 $20.00
Context caching $0.10 $0.20

Introductory prices are the free trial of the API world: pleasant until the calendar turns. Budget on the December column. The pricing page lists batch prices at half of each figure.

How does Google say Gemini 4 Argon behaves as an agent? #

Google reports fewer shortcuts and better resistance to planted instructions, and leaves one security question open. The launch post says that on “coding benchmarks where models have no incentive to hold back, shortcut attempts dropped to just 2%”, and that attack success on the Gray Swan Indirect Prompt Injection benchmark fell to 0.7% “across 15 adaptive attempts”.

The model card is blunter about one risk: “We cannot rule out that Gemini 4 Argon reaches the cybersecurity CCL”. CCL is a risk threshold the model card tests against. The card then says “We are therefore applying safeguards that we assess lower risk to below the cybersecurity CCL level”.

Should you switch your coding agents to Gemini 4 Argon? #

Test it on long jobs first, and do not move everything at once. Our reasons: it tops most of Google’s table, the coding rows are split, and the price doubles on 1 December 2026.

FAQ

When was Gemini 4 Argon released?

Gemini 4 Argon went on general release on 13 October 2026. Google's launch post of that date says it is now globally available for developers and enterprises. The Verge reported the first reveal on 30 September 2026, when access was limited to a set of trusted cyber defenders.

How much does Gemini 4 Argon cost?

Google's pricing page, read on 13 Oct 2026, lists Gemini 4 Argon at $2.00 input and $10.00 output per 1M tokens through 30 November 2026, then $4.00 input and $20.00 output from 1 December 2026. Output includes thinking tokens. Batch prices are half of each figure.

Is Gemini 4 Argon free?

No. Google's pricing page says Gemini 4 Argon is not available on the Free tier or on the Google AI Pro plan. Google's launch post lists the paid tier of the Gemini API, Google AI Ultra subscribers in AI Studio and Antigravity, and Google AI Ultra and Pro subscribers in the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search.

What is the Gemini 4 Argon context window?

Google's model card lists a combined input and output context window of up to 1M tokens. Google's models page describes a shared 1M context window and long decodes up to 1M tokens. The API model ID is gemini-4-argon.

Is Gemini 4 Argon better than Claude Opus 5.5 at coding?

It depends on the test, on the figures Google published. Google's table has Gemini 4 Argon ahead on DeepSWE v1.1 (77.9% against 74.2%) and Vibe Code Bench (91.9% against 90.3%), and Claude Opus 5.5 ahead on Terminal-bench 4.0 (66.4% against 57.4%) and FrontierSWE v2 (62.3% against 55.0%).
