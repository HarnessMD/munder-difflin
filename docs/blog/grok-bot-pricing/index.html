Grok Bot Pricing in Nov 2026: Premium $30, Super $100, Ultra $200

Grok Bot pricing, checked 16 Nov 2026: x.ai lists Premium at $30, Super at $100 and Ultra at $200 a month, plus Teams and Enterprise. No free plan listed.

CG Chaitanya Giri · Sep 26, 2026 · 4 min read Comparisons
TL;DR

On 16 Nov 2026, x.ai's Grok Bot page lists Premium at $30 a month, Super at $100 and Ultra at $200, each billed monthly. Teams and Enterprise plans include it too. No free plan is listed. If you only need work agents, you can run them free on your own computer.

Grok Bot costs $30, $100 or $200 a month on the Premium, Super and Ultra plans that x.ai’s Grok Bot page lists on 16 Nov 2026, and Teams and Enterprise plans include it too. Checked 16 Nov 2026. No free plan is listed.

Grok Bot is SpaceXSI’s team of always on AI agents, called Bots. They sign into your apps, do the work on a cloud computer and keep going with your laptop shut. Bloomberg reported 418,000 weekly users a month after launch. This page listed SuperGrok and Cursor plans in September; the Grok Bot page now names neither.

If what you want is agents for your work, there is also a free route. Munder Difflin is free and open source: a desktop app that runs a team of AI agents for all kinds of knowledge work, coding included, on your own computer. It uses the AI engines you already have. More on it at the end.

Every plan that includes Grok Bot #

Grok Bot launched in beta on 11 August 2026. On 26 August SpaceXSI opened it to more plans, including SuperGrok and Cursor Pro. Grok Bot 1.0 is dated 2 Nov 2026, and the Grok Bot page now says “Grok Bot is out of beta.” Here is what that page lists as of 16 Nov 2026.

Plan Price, 16 Nov 2026 What the page lists
Premium $30 / month, 16 Nov 2026 Grok Bot’s own computer, routines on a schedule, weekly Grok Bot usage
Super $100 / month, 16 Nov 2026 Everything in Premium, 3.5x Premium usage, priority model access at peak times
Ultra $200 / month, 16 Nov 2026 Everything in Super, 12x Premium usage, earliest access to all products and models
Teams See the Teams tab on the Grok Bot page Included, the page says
Enterprise Contact sales Included, the page says

The page bills all three individual plans monthly and adds: “Already on an eligible Premium, Super, Ultra, Teams, or Enterprise plan? Grok Bot is included.” The 1.0 post splits two features by plan: “Team Bots are available on Team and Enterprise plans. Meetings are available on Team, Enterprise, and Ultra plans.” x.ai’s FAQ adds that it comes “with legacy Cursor Pro, Pro+, Ultra, and self-serve Teams”.

Is Grok Bot free? #

No, not as a plan: the lowest price on the Grok Bot page on 16 Nov 2026 is Premium at $30 a month. x.ai’s FAQ says “Plus and Free don’t include it”, though “Some accounts can try Grok Bot before they pay”: “The trial is a usage credit, and a 7-day window also applies.”

Does Grok Bot eat into your plan’s usage? #

Yes on Premium, Super and Ultra: on 16 Nov 2026 x.ai’s FAQ says “Grok Bot draws from the same weekly usage pool as Cursor and the Grok apps”. On 26 August SpaceXSI had said it “comes with its own usage, separate from your Grok and Cursor plans”. On 16 Nov 2026 the Grok Bot page lists “Weekly Grok Bot usage included” on Premium, “3.5x Premium usage” on Super and “12x Premium usage” on Ultra. It gives multipliers, not task counts.

The same page says Grok Bot subscriptions come with weekly usage included, “with additional usage billed based on token cost.” At launch, eesel’s review noted there was no spend cap on extra usage. If you turn on paid extra usage, keep an eye on it.

What you get for the money #

Premium covers the core product. The Grok Bot page lists “Grok Bot’s own computer”, “Signs into your tools”, “Routines on a schedule” and “Works where you work: desktop, iOS, Slack, Teams and more”. Super and Ultra add usage and priority or earliest access. Team Bots and meetings depend on the plan, as the table shows.

What you do not get at any price:

  • A choice of model. “Grok Bot picks the model for each task, and there is no model picker”, x.ai’s FAQ says.
  • Walls between Bots. The Grok Bot page says “Every Bot has its own computer”, but the FAQ says “Every Bot on your account uses one persistent cloud computer” and “Do not use separate Bots as a security boundary.”

We cover what those limits mean in our Grok Bot alternatives guide.

Which plan should you pick? #

Start with Premium at $30 a month (16 Nov 2026) unless you need more usage, meetings or Team Bots.

  • You want to try Grok Bot on your own. Premium, the lowest price on the page.
  • You will run several Bots all day. Super or Ultra. Super lists 3.5x Premium usage and Ultra 12x.
  • You want a Bot in your calls. Ultra, Teams or Enterprise, the plans the 1.0 post names for meetings.
  • You are buying for a team. Teams or Enterprise, the plans with Team Bots.

The free route for work agents #

If your Bots would do research, writing, ops or code, compare running the agents yourself.

Munder Difflin’s agents run on engines such as Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI, take work in from Slack and webhooks, and use MCP servers, skills and plugins.

If you already pay for a Claude or ChatGPT plan, the agents run on that. To spend nothing on tokens, run it on open models.

The honest trade: Munder Difflin will not do errands like booking travel. Its agents run on your computer, or in its Cloud Office with your computer off. For overnight work, a Mac mini that stays awake does the job for a one time cost.

FAQ

How much does Grok Bot cost?

On 16 Nov 2026 x.ai's Grok Bot page prices it at $30 a month on Premium, $100 on Super and $200 on Ultra, each billed monthly. It says Teams and Enterprise plans include it too, and Enterprise customers contact sales. Extra usage is "billed based on token cost".

Is Grok Bot free?

Not as a plan. On 16 Nov 2026 x.ai's Grok Bot page starts at Premium, $30 a month, and lists no free tier. x.ai's FAQ says "Some accounts can try Grok Bot before they pay" and "The trial is a usage credit, and a 7-day window also applies."

What is the cheapest way to get Grok Bot?

Premium at $30 a month, the lowest price on x.ai's Grok Bot page on 16 Nov 2026. x.ai's FAQ says it also comes "with legacy Cursor Pro, Pro+, Ultra, and self-serve Teams".

Does Grok Bot use up my Cursor or SuperGrok usage?

On Premium, Super and Ultra, yes: on 16 Nov 2026 x.ai's FAQ says "Grok Bot draws from the same weekly usage pool as Cursor and the Grok apps". On 26 Aug 2026 SpaceXSI had said it "comes with its own usage, separate from your Grok and Cursor plans".

Is there a free alternative to Grok Bot?

For work agents, Munder Difflin is free and open source. It runs a team of agents on your own computer with engines you already pay for, or with a free local model. OpenClaw and Hermes Agent are free, open source personal assistants.
