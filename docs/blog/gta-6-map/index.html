GTA 6 Map: Leonida, Vice City and How Big It Is Next to GTA 5

The GTA 6 map is Leonida, Rockstar's take on Florida. Its six regions in Rockstar's words, its size next to GTA 5, and how to explore it spoiler free.

CG Chaitanya Giri · Nov 19, 2026 · 5 min read Guides

The GTA 6 map is Leonida, a fictional state that Rockstar researched in Florida, and Edge reported on 29 Oct 2026 that it is roughly twice the size of GTA 5’s map. Rockstar names six regions, from Vice City to Mount Kalaga, and has published no area in square miles. Checked 19 Nov 2026.

Grand Theft Auto VI launches on 19 Nov 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, per Rockstar’s Newswire. For the rest of launch week, start at our GTA 6 hub.

Where is GTA 6 set? #

GTA 6 is set in Leonida, Rockstar’s fictional state. Rockstar’s Trailer 1 post (5 Dec 2023) says the game “heads to the state of Leonida, home to the neon-soaked streets of Vice City and beyond”. Its Only in Leonida page says “the sunshine state”, and its GTA VI page heads the synopsis “Vice City, USA.”

The Florida link comes from reporting. Edge (29 Oct 2026) reports that in 2015 Rockstar North’s Rob Nelson “led a small group travelling to Miami to consider what a return to Vice City might look and feel like”. Polygon’s review (18 Nov 2026) calls it “the Florida-inspired state of Leonida”, and Eurogamer’s review says “Leonida is just a heightened Florida.”

Which city is GTA 6 based on? Vice City, which AP called “the Miami-inspired Vice City” on 5 Dec 2023.

Times the word Florida appears on Rockstar’s GTA VI page and Only in Leonida page, read 19 Nov 2026: 0. “Sunshine state” is doing a lot of quiet work.

What are the regions of the GTA 6 map? #

Rockstar names six on Only in Leonida, under Places, in this order: Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga. The last column names a real place only where an outlet does. Read 19 Nov 2026.

Region What Rockstar says Real place it echoes, per a source
Vice City “the sun and fun capital of America”, with Ocean Beach, Little Cuba, the Tisha-Wocka flea market and VC Port Miami (AP, 5 Dec 2023)
Leonida Keys “Life in this tropical archipelago isn’t flashy but it’s easy.” The Florida Keys (Polygon, 18 Nov 2026; CBS12, 19 Jun 2026)
Grassrivers “this primordial expanse”, where “The gators may be the most famous attraction” The Everglades (CBS12, 19 Jun 2026: “Grassrivers evokes the Everglades”)
Port Gellhorn “Cheap motels” and “empty strip malls won’t bring the tourists back” None named in what we read
Ambrosia “In the heart of Leonida”, where “The Allied Crystal sugar refinery provides the jobs” None named
Mount Kalaga “A national landmark up against the state’s northern border” None named

Polygon adds that, unlike real Florida, the north of Leonida “actually features topography of differing elevations”. Actual hills, in other words. Ocean Beach also turns up in the preorder pack (editions and price).

How big is the GTA 6 map compared to GTA 5? #

Roughly twice the size, Edge reports, but Rockstar has published no area in square miles or kilometres. Three sourced yardsticks, three different rulers:

  • Twice GTA V. Edge, 29 Oct 2026: “The map is roughly twice the size of GTAV’s”.
  • GTA IV, GTA V and RDR2 combined. Same piece: “Rockstar says its total landmass, including Vice City and the surrounding areas, is nearly as large as the playable maps of GTAIV, GTAV and RDR2 combined.”
  • Three times RDR2. tbreak reports (28 Aug 2026), quoting GameSpot on a New York Times interview: “The overall map, the studio said, is three times as large as the playable area of its previous game, Red Dead Redemption 2.”

As tbreak put it on 1 Nov 2026: “Rockstar hasn’t published an area measurement to tie them together.” Rockstar’s 2023 Trailer 1 post promised “the biggest, most immersive evolution of the Grand Theft Auto series yet”, which is a mood, not a measurement.

Why are fan maps of GTA 6 only estimates? #

Because there is no official area to check them against. A fan map that quotes square miles picks its own scale, from road widths, car lengths or a landmark, and area grows with the square of that scale: a 10% scale error becomes about 21% in area (plain arithmetic: 1.1 squared is 1.21).

Before launch, fan maps could only draw on trailers, screenshots and leaks; Metro reports (4 Nov 2026) that Rockstar takes down leaked clips. Our view: a fan square mile figure is an estimate; Edge’s “roughly twice” is the sourced one.

How do you explore Leonida without spoilers? #

Use Rockstar’s own material and avoid leaks and story videos:

  1. Read Only in Leonida. One paragraph per region, no plot.
  2. Get the official printable map. Rockstar’s 3 Nov Newswire post says: “On November 19 we will be adding an official printable Grand Theft Auto VI cover art poster and map of Leonida as a free digital download to the official site.” A double sided physical version follows from the Rockstar Store “shortly after launch”. Checked on 19 Nov 2026 at 04:18 IST, the downloads page did not list the map yet.
  3. Skip anything labelled leak. None of it is official.
  4. Prefer text guides to video. Rockstar removes endings and isolated cinematics, tbreak reports (4 Nov 2026), but only after the upload. Read Rockstar’s policy page.

How does the in game map screen work? Rockstar’s pages we read on 19 Nov 2026 do not say, so this guide does not guess.

FAQ

Is GTA 6 based on Florida?

Leonida is a fictional state, and Rockstar's own pages call it "the sunshine state" without naming Florida. Edge reported on 29 Oct 2026 that Rockstar North's Rob Nelson led a research trip to Miami in 2015 to study Florida culture, and Polygon's review calls Leonida Florida inspired.

How big is the GTA 6 map compared to GTA 5?

Edge reported on 29 Oct 2026 that the map is roughly twice the size of GTA V's, and that Rockstar says its landmass is nearly as large as the playable maps of GTA IV, GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 combined. Rockstar has published no area in square miles or kilometres, so any exact figure is an estimate.

Which city is GTA 6 based on?

Vice City, the game's main city, which AP described as Miami inspired when Trailer 1 came out on 5 Dec 2023. It is one of six regions Rockstar names in Leonida.

Is there an official GTA 6 map?

Rockstar said on 3 Nov 2026 that it would add a printable map of Leonida as a free digital download on its site on 19 Nov, with a double sided physical poster from the Rockstar Store shortly after launch. When we checked early on 19 Nov 2026, the downloads page did not list the map yet.

How many regions are in GTA 6?

Rockstar's Only in Leonida page names six: Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga. Other place names, such as Stockyard and Lake Leonida, appear in Rockstar's Ultimate Edition list without a region attached.
