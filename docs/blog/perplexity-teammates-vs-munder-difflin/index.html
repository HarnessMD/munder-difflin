Perplexity Teammates vs Munder Difflin: Hosted Agents or Your Own Computer (Nov 2026)

Perplexity Teammates are hosted agents for ongoing work on Max. Munder Difflin runs a team of agents on your own computer. Compared, checked 7 Nov 2026.

MD Chaitanya Giri · Nov 7, 2026 · 5 min read Comparisons

Our view: pick Perplexity Teammates if you already pay for Max and want hosted agents that watch ongoing work in their own browsers, and Munder Difflin if you want a team of agents for all kinds of knowledge work, coding included, on your own computer. Checked 7 Nov 2026.

Munder Difflin is free and open source: a desktop app that runs a team of AI agents for all kinds of knowledge work, coding included, on your own computer. Other matchups live in the Comparisons hub.

What are Perplexity Teammates? #

Teammates are hosted agents inside Perplexity Computer that own ongoing work. Perplexity’s launch post, dated 4 Nov 2026 and read 7 Nov 2026, says: “Today, we’re launching teammates, personal agents that can take on that long-running work.” It adds: “They plan and coordinate projects, switch gears as priorities change, and present results for review.”

A teammate is a manager as much as a worker. The post says “A teammate coordinates Computer sessions and other teammates to keep projects moving.” Teammates “remember project goals, task history, and shared files across runs”, and you connect “plugins such as Slack, GitHub, or Gmail”. There is also an en-GB version of the same post.

The headline feature is follow through. “Teammates monitor ongoing work and follow up as things change. They investigate and act without waiting for a fresh query.” In office terms, that is the colleague who replies before you chase.

What is Munder Difflin? #

Munder Difflin is a desktop app where a team of agents takes research, writing, ops and code, on your own computer. Agents run while your computer runs. Its README, read 7 Nov 2026, describes “A GOD orchestrator you talk to. It routes tasks, adjudicates traffic, and escalates only what needs a human.”

Work can start outside the app. The README says: “message a channel or POST a webhook; Michael can spawn an ephemeral worker, reply in-thread, and tear it down.” Agents run on engines such as Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI, and pick up tools through MCP servers, skills and plugins. A Cloud Office and a mobile app are coming soon. Setup is in how to install and use Munder Difflin.

What do Perplexity Teammates cost? #

Teammates come with Perplexity Max. The Teammates page, read 7 Nov 2026, says that after a promotional grant, “teammates use the credits that are already included with your Max plan.” Perplexity’s Help Center, read 7 Nov 2026, says “Perplexity Max costs $200/monthly or $2000/annually.” The pricing page, read the same day, shows Max at “$167 /month when billed annually”. The launch post says “Free and Pro subscribers can upgrade in Settings > Account > Subscription.”

Where is Perplexity Teammates stronger? #

It is stronger at hosted work that keeps going on its own. All four points come from Perplexity’s pages, read 7 Nov 2026.

  • Hosted and proactive today. The Teammates page says “Proactive by design, they run on automations and keep moving work forward on your behalf.” Munder Difflin’s agents run while your computer runs, and its Cloud Office is coming soon.
  • A browser per teammate. The launch post says “Each teammate runs in a separate environment, with browser sessions separate from other teammates.” You can “take over the desktop, or redirect a teammate as it works.”
  • Memory across runs. Teammates “remember project goals, task history, and shared files across runs”, and each new task “builds on the work already done.”
  • Business examples in the launch post. The post walks through marketing, sales, support and operations teammates, such as one that “follows up on missing items when a deadline is 72 hours away”.

Where is Munder Difflin different? #

Munder Difflin runs on your own computer, with the engines you choose. Inside Perplexity, the product “selects the best one per query” unless you set a preferred model. Munder Difflin runs engines such as Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI.

It is also free and open source, while Teammates sit behind Max. One orchestrator runs the floor: the README calls it “A GOD orchestrator you talk to.” Work can come in through Slack or a webhook, and tools through MCP servers, skills and plugins. The trade is plain: agents run while your computer runs, and the hosted Cloud Office is coming soon.

Who should pick which? #

Our view: it depends on where you want the agents to live. Pick Perplexity Teammates if you already pay for Max, live in the browser, and want hosted agents that watch ongoing work and follow up with nobody at the keyboard.

Pick Munder Difflin if you want a team of agents on research, writing, ops and code on your own computer, running engines you choose, with work arriving from Slack or webhooks. For other matchups, read Orchestrator.inc vs Munder Difflin or our Claude Cowork alternatives roundup.
