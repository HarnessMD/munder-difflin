Turnstone Alternatives: 5 Tools Like Turnstone for AI Teammates (Nov 2026)

Turnstone runs AI teammates on the subscriptions you already pay for, on a Mac. Five alternatives, from Claude Cowork to OpenClaw, checked 11 Nov 2026.

MD Chaitanya Giri · Nov 11, 2026 · 5 min read Comparisons

Our view: Claude Cowork is the closest Turnstone alternative for most people, because it runs AI teammates for everyday work on a Claude plan you may already pay for. Turnstone is a Mac app that runs AI agents on AI plans you already pay for. Checked 11 Nov 2026.

Munder Difflin is free and open source: a desktop app that runs a team of AI agents for all kinds of knowledge work, coding included, on your own computer. Our pinned Herdr alternatives guide and the Comparisons hub cover the wider field.

What is Turnstone? #

Turnstone is a Mac app for running a team of AI agents. Its facts page, read 11 Nov 2026, says “Each Agent has its own job, its own model and its own memory.” Its Y Combinator page lists it in the Winter 2026 batch as “The best model-agnostic Grokbot alternative”.

You start with a Chief of Staff, which the home page says “sets up an Agent for each part of the job.” Every Agent shares one memory, the Brain, which “is stored as plain files on your Mac that you can open and correct.” The home page links comparison pages against 12 rivals, so this page returns the favour.

How does Turnstone use your subscriptions? #

It signs in with plans you already pay for and can move an agent to another one when a plan runs out. The home page says “Sign in with ChatGPT, Claude, Grok or Cursor, or use an API key.” The YC page adds: “When a subscription runs out, your Agent can automatically move to another connected subscription with available capacity.”

Is Turnstone free? #

Yes on a Mac, and its Cloud add on is paid and not out yet. Turnstone’s pricing page, read 11 Nov 2026, lists Free at $0 “forever” with your own subscription or API key. Cloud is marked “Coming soon” at $49 per month, with a waitlist, and on 11 Nov 2026 the home page offered the first 1,000 people a free first month.

Why look for a Turnstone alternative? #

Mostly for platform, source code or cloud. The facts page lists “Windows, Linux, Android” as “Not available”, and Turnstone Cloud, the iPhone app and the web app as “Coming soon”. Turnstone’s own OpenClaw comparison answers the open source row with “No. Closed source.”

None of this makes it a bad tool. This list is for people comparing tools like Turnstone before they pick.

1. Claude Cowork: the closest swap on a Claude plan #

Cowork does Turnstone’s job inside Claude. Anthropic’s Cowork guide, read 11 Nov 2026, says “Cowork is available to paid Claude plans (Pro, Max, Ultra, Team, Enterprise) only.” It adds “Work continues if you close your laptop”, which Turnstone Cloud has not shipped yet. Claude’s pricing page listed Pro at $20 a month billed monthly on 11 Nov 2026. It suits people who already pay for Claude; see Claude Cowork alternatives.

2. Munder Difflin: free, open source, three systems #

Munder Difflin is ours, and it runs on Windows and Linux as well as macOS. Its README, read 11 Nov 2026, says it “works with the subscriptions you already pay for, on their hourly limits” and offers “Download 0.5.6 for macOS, Windows or Linux”. Work can arrive from Slack or a webhook, and agents add tools through MCP servers, skills and plugins. A Cloud Office and a mobile app are coming soon. It suits research, writing, ops and code. Setup is in how to install and use Munder Difflin.

3. Grok Bot: the one Turnstone measures itself against #

Grok Bot is xAI’s team of cloud agents. xAI’s launch post of 11 Aug 2026 says “Grok Bot is your team of always-on agents. They have their own computer”. Cursor’s plans page, read 11 Nov 2026, says “On The X Subscription, Grok Bot is included on Premium, Super, and Ultra”. It suits people already on those plans; see Grok Bot alternatives.

4. Perplexity Teammates: if you live in Perplexity #

Teammates are Perplexity’s agents for ongoing work. Its launch post of 4 Nov 2026 says “Teammates are initially launching on the web and soon on iOS for Perplexity Max subscribers.” On 11 Nov 2026 the pricing page listed the plan with “Maximum Computer usage” at $167 a month billed annually. It suits people already working in Perplexity. We compare it in Perplexity Teammates vs Munder Difflin.

5. OpenClaw: if you want open source in your chat apps #

OpenClaw is a free assistant that lives in your chat apps. Its README, read 11 Nov 2026, calls it “an open-source AI assistant that runs on your own computer and meets you in the channels you already use”, with “no paid tier, hosted service, or token”. It is MIT licensed, and v2026.11.3 came out on 10 Nov 2026. Our view: it suits people happy to set up a Gateway. See OpenClaw alternatives.

How to choose #

Our view: pick Claude Cowork if you pay for Claude and want work to go on with your laptop closed. Pick Munder Difflin for a free, open source team of agents on macOS, Windows or Linux. Pick Grok Bot if you already have Premium or higher on The X Subscription, Perplexity Teammates if you already pay for its top plan, and OpenClaw for MIT code in your chat apps. Or stay on Turnstone if you work on a Mac and want several AI plans behind one team.

FAQ

What is Turnstone?

Turnstone is a Mac app for running a team of AI agents on the AI plans you already pay for. Its facts page, read 11 Nov 2026, says "Each Agent has its own job, its own model and its own memory." It is in the Y Combinator Winter 2026 batch.

Is Turnstone free?

On a Mac, yes. Its pricing page, read 11 Nov 2026, lists Free at $0 forever when you bring your own subscription or API key. Turnstone Cloud is marked coming soon at $49 a month, and the home page offers the first 1,000 people a free first month.

Does Turnstone run on Windows?

Not today. Its facts page, read 11 Nov 2026, lists Windows, Linux and Android as "Not available", and Cloud, an iPhone app and a web app as "Coming soon". Claude Cowork, Munder Difflin and OpenClaw all name Windows on their own pages.

Is Turnstone open source?

No. Turnstone's own OpenClaw comparison page, read 11 Nov 2026, answers its open source row with "No. Closed source." OpenClaw and Munder Difflin are MIT licensed.

What is the best free Turnstone alternative?

Our view: OpenClaw if you want an assistant in your chat apps, and Munder Difflin if you want a desktop team of agents for research, writing, ops and code. Both are free and MIT licensed, per their pages read 11 Nov 2026.
