Rowboat Alternatives: 5 Tools Like Rowboat for an AI Coworker (Nov 2026)

Rowboat is a free, open source AI coworker with a local knowledge graph and Spaces for agent teams. Five alternatives, checked 13 Nov 2026.

We make Munder Difflin, which is free and open source: a desktop app that runs a team of AI agents for all kinds of knowledge work, coding included, on your own computer. See also our pinned Herdr alternatives guide, the Comparisons hub and open source AI agents.

What is Rowboat? #

Rowboat is a free, open source set of apps for working with AI agents, made by Rowboat Labs. Its README, read 13 Nov 2026, leads with “Your agent team, in one group chat.” Its Y Combinator page calls it an “Open-source AI coworker with memory” that “builds a local knowledge graph from your work”. The licence is Apache 2.0, v1.0.9 came out on 11 Nov 2026, and the repo showed 18,188 stars on 13 Nov 2026.

How does Rowboat run? #

It runs on the web, on iPhone, and as a desktop app for Mac, Windows and Linux, per the README. The desktop app “is a personal AI assistant that runs on your machine”, with an email client, a meeting note taker and a code mode for Claude Code or Codex. It connects to Gmail, Calendar and Drive, and to other tools through MCP servers or Composio.

What are Rowboat’s knowledge graph and Spaces? #

The knowledge graph is the desktop app’s memory, and Spaces are shared chats for people and agents. The README says Rowboat “indexes email, meetings, slack and assistant conversations” into a backlinked graph, and “everything lives on your machine as plain Markdown”.

In a Space, you invite collaborators and agents such as Hermes, OpenClaw, Claude Code or Codex, approve any new agent they spin up, and the agents “mention each other, hand off work, and build on the same whiteboards, docs and task lists.” Spaces run on Harbor, an open source server you can host yourself or let Rowboat host.

How much does Rowboat cost? #

Rowboat is free, per its README read 13 Nov 2026: “Free. Bring your own agents or spin up hosted ones.”

Why look for a Rowboat alternative? #

Our view: for stability, a simpler setup, or a plan you already pay for. The README says parts of the Spaces protocol are “deliberately still v0” and adds “Expect breaking changes while we dogfood.” Optional voice and Exa search need your own Deepgram, ElevenLabs and Exa keys. Otherwise, this list is for people comparing tools like Rowboat.

1. AnythingLLM: the closest match to Rowboat’s memory #

AnythingLLM is a free, open source AI app that learns from your files on your computer. Its home page, read 13 Nov 2026, says “A private AI assistant that runs entirely on your computer.” The README lists “Automatic & User Managed Memories”, and v1.19.0 came out on 26 Oct 2026. Cloud plans started at $50 a month on 13 Nov 2026. We think it suits people who want local memory without a group chat.

2. Munder Difflin: a team of agents on your own computer #

We build this one. Its README, read 13 Nov 2026, says “each agent gets long-term memory, a mailbox, and a desk on a 2D office floor”. Work arrives from Slack or a webhook, and agents gain tools through MCP servers, skills and plugins. Agents run on your computer, or in our Cloud Office, always on, with your computer off; a mobile app is coming soon. We think it suits several agents sharing research, writing, ops and code. Setup is in how to install and use Munder Difflin.

3. Eigent: an agent workforce on your desktop #

Eigent is an open source Cowork desktop for Mac, Windows and Linux where several agents split one job. Its README, read 13 Nov 2026, lists a “Multi-Agent Workforce” under Apache 2.0, and v1.0.7 came out on 30 Oct 2026. The pricing page listed a Free plan with your own keys, and Plus at $19.99 a month, on 13 Nov 2026. We think it suits people who want agents to divide one big task.

4. AionUi: one window for many agent CLIs #

AionUi is a free desktop app where AI agents work on your computer. Its README, read 13 Nov 2026, calls it “a Cowork platform where AI agents work alongside you on your computer”, under Apache 2.0. Its WebUI opens it from a phone. Commits landed through 5 Nov 2026. We think it suits people who already run Claude Code or Codex; see AionUi alternatives.

5. Claude Cowork: if you already pay for Claude #

Cowork hands Claude complex tasks to finish on your behalf. Anthropic’s help page, read 13 Nov 2026, says it “runs your sessions remotely in the cloud” and that projects keep “their own files, links, instructions, and memory”. It needs a paid plan, and Claude’s pricing page listed Pro at $20 if billed monthly on 13 Nov 2026. We think it suits people who already pay for Claude; see Claude Cowork alternatives.

How to choose #

Pick by what it should remember and who works in it. Our view:

  • AnythingLLM for one private assistant that learns your documents.
  • Munder Difflin for several agents with their own memory, doing research, writing, ops and code.
  • Eigent for agents splitting one large task.
  • AionUi if you already run several agent CLIs.
  • Claude Cowork if you already pay for Claude.
  • Rowboat for a knowledge graph of your email and a shared agent chat. It is the only pick with oars.

FAQ

What is Rowboat?

Rowboat, from Rowboat Labs, is a free and open source set of apps for working with AI agents. Its desktop app builds a local knowledge graph from your email, meetings and Slack, and its Spaces put people and agents in one group chat. Its Y Combinator page, read 13 Nov 2026, lists it in the Summer 2024 batch.

Is Rowboat free and open source?

Yes. Its README, read 13 Nov 2026, says "Free and open source." and the licence is Apache 2.0.

Does Rowboat work with Claude Code?

Yes. Its README, read 13 Nov 2026, says you can connect agents you already run, like Claude Code, to a Space. The desktop app's code mode also drives Claude Code or Codex.

What is the closest Rowboat alternative?

Our view: AnythingLLM, because it is also a free, open source desktop app that runs on your computer and keeps memories about you and your workspace. Its README, read 13 Nov 2026, carries an MIT licence. Munder Difflin is the pick if you want a team of agents rather than one assistant.

Where does Rowboat keep my data?

On your computer, for the desktop app. Its README, read 13 Nov 2026, says "All personal data is stored locally as plain Markdown". Spaces live on a Harbor server, either hosted by Rowboat or run by you.
