GTA 6 Review Scores: Metacritic, OpenCritic and What Critics Actually Said

GTA 6 review scores on 19 Nov 2026: Metacritic 99 from 150 critics, OpenCritic 99. How each average works, 7 critics quoted, and is it worth it.

CG Chaitanya Giri · Nov 19, 2026 · 5 min read News

GTA 6 review scores are 99 on Metacritic (PlayStation 5, from 150 critic reviews) and a 99 Top Critic Average on OpenCritic, which lists 88 critic reviews. Checked 19 Nov 2026.

We last read both pages at 04:41 IST on 19 Nov 2026 (23:11 UTC on 18 Nov). No spoilers below. Everything else: the GTA 6 hub.

What are the GTA 6 review scores right now? #

It is 99 on both big aggregators, as of 19 Nov 2026. Metacritic shows a PlayStation 5 Metascore of 99, “Based on 150 Critic Reviews”, with “100% Positive”. Xbox Series X is also 99, from 14 reviews. The user score reads “tbd”: the game launches on 19 Nov (Rockstar’s Newswire).

OpenCritic shows a 99 Top Critic Average, “99% Critics Recommend” and 88 critic reviews.

Metacritic’s help center says a Metascore can change “sometimes many times within a day”, in an article whose title ends “Am I hallucinating?”

How do Metacritic and OpenCritic calculate the score? #

Metacritic takes a weighted average; OpenCritic takes a plain one. Metacritic’s about page says “The weighted average of those scores results in a single number and color, the Metascore.” Its help center adds: “we assign more importance, or weight, to some critics and publications than others, based on their quality and overall stature.” The weights themselves? Its help article “Can you tell me how each of the different critics are weighted in your formula?” answers, in full: “Absolutely not.”

OpenCritic’s FAQ says “The Top Critic Average is calculated by taking the simple average score of all numeric reviews written by top critics after normalizing the score on a 0-100 scale.” Unscored reviews are “not included in any score calculations”, so Polygon’s unscored review does not touch the 99.

Do this, not that: compare on the same site and platform, and read the review count.

What do critics praise, and what do they complain about? #

The praise is the world; the complaints are frame rate, slow stretches and a familiar core.

  • Praised: detail, freedom, presentation. IGN: “Exquisitely detailed”. TheSixthAxis: “so much more freedom with how to tackle missions”. Push Square: “The audiovisual presentation is a new high for the industry”.
  • Flagged: 30 fps. Rockstar is “targeting 30 frames per second” (Newswire, 10 Nov 2026), and VGC writes that “Minor performance issues don’t dull the sparkle”.
  • Flagged: pace and familiarity. ScreenHub, the 80, found “large sections that are boring and too limited”. SECTOR.sk gave 100 and still wrote that “the core gameplay remains largely familiar”.

On Metacritic that adds up to 150 positive, 0 mixed and 0 negative.

How does GTA 6 compare with GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2? #

On Metacritic, GTA 6 scores two points higher than both. Original platforms, read 19 Nov 2026:

  • GTA VI (PS5): 99 from 150 reviews.
  • GTA V (17 Sep 2013): 97 on Xbox 360 from 58 reviews.
  • Red Dead Redemption 2 (26 Oct 2018): 97 on PS4 from 99 reviews.

On OpenCritic the gap is wider: Red Dead Redemption 2 has a 95 Top Critic Average and GTA V a 92. OpenCritic dates GTA V’s entry 18 Nov 2014, and its FAQ says records before 1 October 2015 “are meant to establish a baseline and not be a 100% accurate historical record.”

The 99 also matches The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time: 99 on Nintendo 64, from 22 reviews, in 1998. GTA 6 got there with almost seven times as many critics.

Is GTA 6 worth it? #

Our view: yes, if you like open worlds and can live with 30 fps, because the evidence is unusually one sided: 150 positive Metacritic reviews and 99% of OpenCritic’s critics recommending it. PlayStation’s US store lists Standard at $79.99 on 19 Nov 2026; the editions and price guide has the rest.

We think three groups should check first:

  • Frame rate fans. Rockstar’s target is 30 fps; the PS5 Pro adds “improved clarity” and ray traced shadows.
  • Online players. Rockstar calls GTA VI “a single player experience” (Newswire, 24 Jun 2026).
  • Busy people. One Rockstar developer’s playthrough took 80 hours, TechRadar reports (28 Aug 2026); see how long GTA 6 takes to beat.

Player reviews are not in yet; if you trust players over critics, wait for the user score.

FAQ

What is GTA 6's Metacritic score?

99 on PlayStation 5 from 150 critic reviews, and 99 on Xbox Series X from 14, when we read Metacritic at 04:41 IST on 19 Nov 2026. All 150 PS5 reviews count as positive. Metacritic says scores for new games can change many times a day, so check the live page.

What did IGN give GTA 6?

IGN's Luke Reilly gave it 10/10 on 18 Nov 2026 and wrote that "Grand Theft Auto 6 is the single most impressive video game I have ever played." GameSpot also gave it 10/10 and Eurogamer 5/5.

What is OpenCritic's Top Critic Average?

OpenCritic's FAQ says it is calculated by taking "the simple average score of all numeric reviews written by top critics after normalizing the score on a 0-100 scale." Reviews without a score do not count. GTA 6 had a Top Critic Average of 99 on 19 Nov 2026, with 88 critic reviews listed.

Is GTA 6 worth the hype?

Our view: for open world fans, yes. Metacritic counted 150 positive reviews and no mixed or negative ones, and 99% of OpenCritic's critics recommend it. Check first if a 30 fps target, a single player game or a very long story is a problem for you.

Does GTA 6 score higher than GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2?

Yes, on both sites. On Metacritic GTA 6 has 99, GTA V 97 and Red Dead Redemption 2 97. On OpenCritic it is 99, 92 and 95, though OpenCritic says its records from before October 2015, which include GTA V, are a baseline rather than an exact history.
