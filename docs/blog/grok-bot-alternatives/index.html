Grok Bot Alternatives: 9 Picks, Hosted or on Your Computer

Grok Bot runs every Bot on one shared cloud computer in the US. Nine alternatives checked on 30 Sep 2026, from ChatGPT dots to open source agents you run.

MD Chaitanya Giri · Sep 14, 2026 · 5 min read Comparisons

The best Grok Bot alternatives are ChatGPT dots for another always on agent with its own cloud computer, Munder Difflin or OpenClaw for agents on your own computer, and Claude Cowork, Gemini Spark or Meta Muse if you live in those apps. Every pick was rechecked on vendor pages on 30 Sep 2026.

Munder Difflin is free and open source: a desktop app that runs a team of AI agents for all kinds of knowledge work, coding included, on your own computer.

What is Grok Bot? #

Grok Bot is a team of AI agents, called Bots, that work on a cloud computer of their own. SpaceXAI announced it in beta on 11 Aug 2026. Bots sign into your tools and ask for approval when needed: a multi agent harness, hosted for you. SpaceXAI’s FAQ says closing the app does not stop a background turn or routine. Cursor’s Grok Bot plans page says access comes with every paid individual Cursor plan and Cursor Teams; Grok Bot pricing has every tier.

Why look for a Grok Bot alternative? #

People leave over where it runs, what each Bot can reach and who picks the model, all set out in SpaceXAI’s security FAQ (read 30 Sep 2026).

It only runs in Cursor’s cloud, in the United States today. No on premises or bring your own image deployments. See why local first matters for AI agents.

Every Bot shares one computer. The security FAQ says to treat its logins and files as every Bot’s, and the Grok Bot FAQ says separate Bots are no security boundary.

Cursor picks the model. Enterprise allowlists exist, but onboarding warns Grok Bot may not follow them. A helper that reads the open web, holds your logins and sends email is the lethal trifecta.

What are the best Grok Bot alternatives? #

Nine, each tagged with where it runs: four in a vendor’s cloud, one hosted with a local mode, and four on hardware you control.

1. ChatGPT dots (OpenAI’s cloud). The closest match, launched 29 Sep 2026, with plugins for over 4,000 apps. See ChatGPT dots alternatives.

2. Munder Difflin (your computer). Munder Difflin is what we make. It is free and open source: a desktop app that runs a team of AI agents for all kinds of knowledge work, coding included, on your own computer. Each agent runs on an engine you choose, such as Claude Code, Codex or Gemini CLI. A Slack message or a webhook can start work, and skills, plugins and MCP servers add what your agents can reach. Agents work while your computer is on; the Cloud Office, where work goes on with your computer off, is coming soon. Start with how to install and use Munder Difflin.

3. ChatGPT Work (OpenAI’s cloud, or your desktop). OpenAI’s Work help page says it runs in the cloud, can use local files in the desktop app and can start on a schedule or a Gmail, Slack or GitHub event.

4. Claude Cowork (Anthropic’s cloud). Anthropic’s Cowork help page says it runs on Anthropic’s servers (in beta) and scheduled tasks don’t need your computer awake.

5. Gemini Spark (Google’s cloud and your Chrome). Google’s Spark help page says it needs Google AI Pro or Ultra and might finish in a remote browser if your device goes off.

6. Meta Muse (Meta’s cloud). On 29 Sep 2026 Meta called Muse “a personal AI agent available in the US and Canada”. Meta’s subscriptions page listed Power at $20 a month and Max at $100 on 30 Sep 2026. See Meta Muse vs Grok Bot.

7. OpenClaw (your computer). OpenClaw is a personal assistant you own, answering in WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, iMessage and 20+ other channels. The catch: tools run straight on your machine in the main session unless you set up sandboxing.

8. Hermes Agent (your computer or server). Hermes Agent from Nous Research is the pick if memory matters most: it curates its own memory and writes skills after complex tasks.

9. NanoClaw (your computer, in containers). NanoClaw is the direct fix for Grok Bot’s shared computer: each agent group gets its own Linux container and sees only the folders you mount.

Is there a free or open source Grok Bot alternative? #

Yes: OpenClaw, Munder Difflin, Hermes Agent and NanoClaw are all free and open source. The software costs nothing, but a model still does. Munder Difflin drives the CLIs you already have, so the Claude or ChatGPT subscription you pay for today does the work. You also need an always on machine, and security is your job.

Gemini Spark vs Grok Bot: which is better? #

Grok Bot is better for most people outside Google’s world, and Spark is better if your day already happens in Gmail and Calendar. Grok Bot runs several Bots on a persistent cloud computer; Spark is one agent, closed to work or school accounts.

Is Grok Bot worth it? #

Grok Bot is worth it if you want almost no setup and are fine with the cloud. Look elsewhere for data outside the United States, a model you choose, or walls between helpers.

  • Pick ChatGPT dots for one always on OpenAI agent.
  • Pick Munder Difflin if you want a team of agents for research, writing, ops or code, each on the engine that suits it, on your own computer.
  • Pick Claude Cowork for cloud reports, files and research.
  • Pick Gemini Spark if you live in Google apps, outside Europe.
  • Pick Meta Muse for a free hosted agent in the US or Canada.
  • Pick OpenClaw, Hermes Agent or NanoClaw to keep everything on your own hardware.

Grok Bot’s shared computer is a bit like Dunder Mifflin’s shared fridge: fine until someone labels nothing and eats your lunch.

FAQ

Is Grok Bot free?

Only as a trial. Cursor's Grok Bot plans page, checked on 30 Sep 2026, describes the trial as a usage credit with a seven day window. After that you need a paid individual Cursor plan, Cursor Teams, or a linked individual SuperGrok, SuperGrok Plus, SuperGrok Heavy or X Premium+ account. SuperGrok Lite does not include it.

What is the best free alternative to Grok Bot?

OpenClaw for a personal assistant in your chat apps, Munder Difflin for a team of AI agents for all kinds of knowledge work, coding included, and Hermes Agent for memory that learns. All three are free and open source and run on hardware you control, but you still pay for the AI model or use a subscription you already have. Meta Muse has a free hosted tier in the US and Canada.

Is Gemini Spark better than Grok Bot?

Only if your work lives in Gmail, Drive and Calendar and you are outside Europe. Spark needs Google AI Pro or Ultra and a personal Google account, and Google excludes the EEA, Switzerland, the UK and Nigeria. Grok Bot has its own always on cloud computer and several Bots, and it comes with paid Cursor plans.

Can you choose which model Grok Bot uses?

No. SpaceXAI's security FAQ says Cursor manages model selection and there is no model picker for customers. Enterprise teams can set a model allowlist, but the same page says enforcement is not guaranteed.

Is Grok Bot the same as Grok on X?

No. Grok on X is the Grok chatbot inside the X app. Grok Bot is a separate agent app whose Bots work on their own cloud computer, sign into your tools and run routines, and SpaceXAI lists the two as separate products.
