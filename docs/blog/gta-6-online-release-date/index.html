GTA 6 Online Release Date: What Rockstar Has Confirmed

There is no GTA 6 Online release date. Rockstar calls GTA VI single player. What that means for friends, PS Plus, Game Pass and crossplay.

CG Chaitanya Giri · Nov 19, 2026 · 5 min read News

There is no GTA 6 Online release date, because Rockstar has announced no online mode: its only word we found, on 24 Jun 2026, is that Grand Theft Auto VI “is a single player experience”. Checked 19 Nov 2026.

The game itself is out today: “Grand Theft Auto VI arrives November 19 to PlayStation 5 and XBOX Series X|S,” Rockstar wrote on 15 Oct 2026. More launch coverage: the GTA 6 hub.

What has Rockstar said about GTA 6 Online? #

Rockstar has said one thing that we found, and it is not a date. Its preorder post of 24 Jun 2026 reads: “Grand Theft Auto VI is a single player experience, coming November 19 to PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.” Sony’s GTA VI page asks itself “Does Grand Theft Auto VI have any multiplayer modes or features?” and answers: “Grand Theft Auto VI is a single-player experience.”

On Rockstar’s Newswire, read 19 Nov 2026, the newest GTA VI post is from 10 Nov 2026, and no GTA VI post we read names an online mode, a beta or a date. A site that prints one is guessing.

One thing looks like an online clue and is not. Digital preorders came with a free month of GTA+, whose perks include a monthly deposit “into your GTA Online character’s bank account”, per Rockstar’s GTA VI page. That is today’s GTA Online, not a GTA VI mode. Sony’s page adds that the month “will continue into a paid monthly subscription”, so cancel it in Subscriptions Management if you only wanted the free part (editions and price).

Can I play GTA 6 with friends at launch? #

No, not inside GTA VI: Sony’s page lists no multiplayer modes or features, so there is nothing to join together. What a group can do tonight:

  • Play GTA Online together. It is still running: Rockstar posted GTA Online news on 5 and 6 Nov 2026, and Take-Two’s results of 9 Nov 2026 list a 14 Jul 2026 update for “PS4, PS5, Xbox One, Xbox Series X|S, PC”.
  • Play side by side. Same night, separate games, one call. Nobody steals your getaway car.
  • Watch each other. Our streaming setup guide covers Rockstar’s footage rules.

Still deciding whether to buy? The review scores landed on 18 Nov 2026.

Do you need PS Plus or Game Pass for GTA 6? #

Our view: not for GTA VI itself, because both plans sell online multiplayer and Sony says GTA VI has none. Sony’s PlayStation Plus page pitches Essential as “join your friends in online multiplayer”. Microsoft’s Game Pass page sells Essential with “online console multiplayer and in-game benefits.” Sony’s GTA VI fine print says “Online features require an account for PlayStation” and names no PlayStation Plus plan.

If you searched for Game Pass Core, it is now Game Pass Essential. Its support page says Games with Gold moved to “XBOX Game Pass Core, which has since transitioned to XBOX Game Pass Essential as of October 1, 2025”, and the compare page lists Ultimate, Premium, Essential and PC Game Pass. No Core.

If your group moves to GTA Online, the plans matter again: Microsoft sells online console multiplayer with Essential, and its compare page opens multiplayer to “every XBOX player” for free to play games.

Is GTA 6 crossplay or cross platform? #

Nothing is announced: no Rockstar or Sony page we read on 19 Nov 2026 mentions crossplay or cross progression, and with no multiplayer there is nothing to cross yet. As for which machines run it: PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, plus Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox One, per Rockstar. No PC version has been announced; Is GTA 6 on PC? has the options.

What happened with GTA Online after GTA 5? #

GTA Online launched 14 days after GTA V, on a date Rockstar had published 33 days before GTA V came out. Rockstar’s post of 15 Aug 2013 said: “Access to Grand Theft Auto Online is free with every retail copy of Grand Theft Auto V and launches on October 1st.” Its post of 17 Sep 2013 opened: “Today we are proud to announce that Grand Theft Auto V is now available worldwide for PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360.”

How do I get GTA 6 Online news first? #

Watch Rockstar’s own pages: every Rockstar date in this post appeared there first.

  1. Bookmark the Newswire. Posts carry a label, and “GTA Online” ones, such as a 6 Nov 2026 free car for GTA+ members, cover today’s GTA Online, not GTA VI.
  2. Set an alert. Google Alerts takes a phrase such as “GTA VI Online” and offers “As-it-happens” delivery.
  3. Use Rockstar’s Subscribe link. It sits in Rockstar’s footer and needs a Rockstar Games account.
  4. Trust primary pages only. A date that is not on rockstargames.com or take2games.com is not a date yet.

FAQ

Is there a GTA 6 Online release date?

No. The only Rockstar statement on it we found is its Newswire post of 24 Jun 2026: "Grand Theft Auto VI is a single player experience". No Rockstar post up to 19 Nov 2026 announces an online mode or a date for one.

Is GTA 6 multiplayer?

Not at launch. Sony's GTA VI page answers "Does Grand Theft Auto VI have any multiplayer modes or features?" with "Grand Theft Auto VI is a single-player experience." Each of you plays your own copy alone.

How can I play with friends on GTA 6 launch night?

Not inside GTA VI. The current GTA Online is still running, with Rockstar Newswire posts about it on 5 and 6 Nov 2026, so that is where a group can play together. Otherwise play side by side on a call, or stream it.

Do I need PS Plus or Game Pass Core to play GTA 6?

Sony and Microsoft sell their Essential plans on online multiplayer, and GTA VI has no multiplayer modes, so we think you need neither for GTA VI itself. Game Pass Core became Game Pass Essential on 1 Oct 2025, per Microsoft's support page. Check the plans again if your group plays GTA Online instead.

Is GTA 6 crossplay or cross platform?

No crossplay is announced. No Rockstar page read on 19 Nov 2026 mentions it, and there is no multiplayer to share across consoles. The game is on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and no PC version has been announced.
