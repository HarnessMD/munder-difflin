GTA 6 Download Size: How Many GB on PS5 and Xbox, and How Long It Takes

GTA 6 download size is up to 212 GB on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The free space you need, download times at common speeds, and what to delete first.

CG Chaitanya Giri · Nov 19, 2026 · 5 min read Guides

The GTA 6 download size is up to 212 GB on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S: Rockstar says the pre load file needs “up to 212 GB depending on your device”, so clear at least 212 GB of free space before you start. Checked 19 Nov 2026.

For the rest of the launch, start at our GTA 6 hub.

How many GB is GTA 6 on PS5 and Xbox? #

Up to 212 GB on both. Rockstar’s pre load post (3 Nov 2026) says “The pre-load file will require up to 212 GB depending on your device”, and Sony’s GTA VI store page asks you to clear “a minimum of 212GB”. No page we read gives a smaller figure for any console, Series S included.

Two traps. The box still downloads: Sony’s GTA VI support page says it “will only contain a game download code” and “A disc will not be included in the box.” That is a lot of cardboard for one code. And tbreak reports (18 Nov 2026) that “the mandatory launch update needs to be installed before you can play at midnight”. We found no published size for it, so leave headroom.

Or skip it: Rockstar’s 10 Nov post puts the game on Xbox Cloud Gaming, “utilizing XBOX Series S graphics settings.”

How much free space do you need on PS5 and Xbox? #

You need 212 GB free on a drive the game can run from, which rules out a plain USB drive.

PS5. Sony’s USB extended storage page says “PlayStation®5 games can’t be played directly from USB extended storage”, so the game goes on the internal drive or an added M.2 SSD. Sony’s M.2 rules: a PCI-Express Gen4x4 M.2 NVMe SSD, 250 GB to 8 TB, 5,500MB/s or faster recommended, a cooling structure such as a heatsink, and no M.2 SATA drives.

Xbox Series X|S. Microsoft’s storage page says Optimized for Xbox Series X|S titles “must be installed on the console’s internal SSD” or the Storage Expansion Card. The card comes in 2TB, 1TB or 512GB and “performs identically to the console’s internal SSD.” Our view: Rockstar’s Xbox release is Series X|S only, so assume the rule applies.

The Series S is the tight one. xbox.com lists a model with a “512GB Custom NVME SSD”, and 212 GB is about 41% of that whole drive (our arithmetic, 19 Nov 2026).

How long does GTA 6 take to download? #

At a steady 100 Mbps, 212 GB takes about 4 hours 43 minutes; at 25 Mbps, about 18 hours 51 minutes. The formula: GB × 8,000 ÷ Mbps = seconds (8 bits a byte, 1,000 megabits a gigabit):

212 GB × 8,000 = 1,696,000 megabits
1,696,000 ÷ 100 Mbps = 16,960 seconds = 4 h 43 min

Real downloads run slower. Microsoft’s slow downloads page warns your speed “might not always match the top speeds that your ISP provides”. Use your console’s connection test result, not your bill.

How do you make the GTA 6 download faster? #

Use a cable, quiet the network, and let the console download while it rests.

  1. Go wired. Microsoft: “If possible, try connecting your console to your home router through a wired connection.” Sony’s error code page says the same. On wireless, Microsoft suggests 5 GHz.
  2. Pause other downloads on your network and close any running game. Microsoft lists both.
  3. Go overnight. Microsoft suggests starting downloads late in the evening to run “during off-peak hours.”
  4. PS5 rest mode. Per Sony’s GTA VI page, turn on Settings > System > Power Saving > Features Available in Rest Mode > Stay Connected to the Internet, then Auto-Install in Rest Mode under Settings > Saved Data and Game/App Settings > Automatic Updates. Sony’s power page notes this draws more power.
  5. Xbox Sleep. Microsoft’s downloadable content page: games install automatically “if you have your power mode set to Sleep and, you’ve enabled Keep my games & apps up to date.”
  6. Stuck? Sony: Pause, then Resume in Downloads/Uploads. Still stuck: Cancel and Delete, then download again.

Staring at the progress bar appears on neither list.

What should you delete first to make room? #

Finished or forgotten games, because both consoles let you download them again. Sony’s storage page says “you can re-download your purchases from your game library whenever you like”, and Microsoft says “make space by uninstalling items you’re not using.”

Our view, in order:

  1. Finished games, under Settings > Storage > Console Storage > Games and Apps on PS5. Own a USB drive? Move them to USB extended storage instead.
  2. Big games you rarely open.
  3. Parts of games. Where a PS5 game shows Customize, Sony lets you delete “add-ons you don’t use or parts of a game that you’ve already finished”.
  4. Screenshots and clips. On PS5, copy them to a USB drive first.

Saves are the smaller worry. Xbox saved data “syncs online when you sign in”; PlayStation Plus members can upload PS5 saves to cloud storage.

FAQ

How many GB is GTA 6 on PS5?

Up to 212 GB. Rockstar's pre load post of 3 Nov 2026 says the file "will require up to 212 GB depending on your device", and PlayStation's store page asks you to clear a minimum of 212GB. Leave extra room for the launch update, which tbreak reports must be installed before you can play.

Is GTA 6 smaller on Xbox Series S?

No smaller size has been published. Rockstar gives one figure, up to 212 GB, and the pages we read on 19 Nov 2026 list nothing separate for the Series S. On the 512GB Series S that is about 41% of the whole drive, so clear space first.

How long does GTA 6 take to download?

It depends on your line. As plain arithmetic, 212 GB takes about 4 hours 43 minutes at a steady 100 Mbps and about 18 hours 51 minutes at 25 Mbps. Real downloads can run slower than your plan's top speed, so put your console's connection test result into GB × 8,000 ÷ Mbps = seconds.

Can I play GTA 6 from an external USB drive?

Not on PS5: Sony says PS5 games can't be played directly from USB extended storage, so you copy them back first. On Xbox Series X|S, Microsoft says Optimized for Xbox Series X|S titles must be installed on the internal SSD or a Storage Expansion Card.

Does the boxed copy of GTA 6 skip the download?

No. PlayStation's GTA VI support page says the boxed version holds only a download code and no disc, and Rockstar says code in box copies exist for both consoles. You download the full game either way.
