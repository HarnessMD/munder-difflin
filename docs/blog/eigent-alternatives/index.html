Eigent Alternatives: 5 Tools for Teams of AI Agents (Nov 2026)

Eigent runs a workforce of AI agents on your desktop. We compare 5 alternatives by how they run, licence and price, as of 14 Nov 2026.

MD Chaitanya Giri · Nov 14, 2026 · 5 min read Comparisons

We make Munder Difflin, which is free and open source: a desktop app that runs a team of AI agents for all kinds of knowledge work, coding included, on your own computer. More lists sit in the Comparisons hub and our OmniAgent alternatives.

What is Eigent? #

Eigent is an open source desktop app that runs AI agents on your computer, built on CAMEL-AI’s open source project. Its README, read 14 Nov 2026, calls it “the open source Cowork desktop application”.

What is Eigent’s workforce? #

The workforce is a group of agents that split one task and work on its parts at once. The README says you can “Scale to multiple specialized agents that divide work, collaborate in parallel, and execute complex multi-step workflows together.” The docs add: “If a task gets stuck or encounters uncertainty, Eigent will automatically request human input.” Automations start agents on a schedule, a webhook or an app event.

How does Eigent run, and which models does it use? #

It runs as a desktop app for macOS, Windows and Linux, per its download page on 14 Nov 2026. The README recommends local deployment with “no cloud account required”, and local models through “vLLM, Ollama, LM Studio, etc.” On the pricing page, the Free plan covers “BYOK and local models” and lists 16 supported models. Plus adds Claude Sonnet 5, Claude Fable 5.1, Claude Opus 5.5 and Grok 4.7.

How much does Eigent cost, and is it open source? #

The app is free and Apache 2.0, and the paid plans sell monthly task credits. On 14 Nov 2026 the pricing page listed Plus at $19.99 a month with 2,000 task credits, and Pro at $99.99 a month with 10,000. Teams read “Coming Soon”. The GitHub repo had 15,818 stars on 14 Nov 2026, and v1.0.7 came out on 30 Oct 2026.

Why look for an Eigent alternative? #

Our view: look elsewhere if you need a shared team plan today or would rather write code. Eigent’s Teams plan, with “shared chats, commands, and rules”, read “Coming Soon” on 14 Nov 2026. Otherwise, this list is for people comparing tools like Eigent before they pick.

1. AionUi: the closest match to Eigent #

AionUi is a free desktop app where a leader agent splits your request across teammate agents. Its README, read 14 Nov 2026, says “a Leader agent receives your instructions, breaks them into subtasks, and delegates to Teammate agents”, which “execute in parallel”. It also brings in agents you already run, such as Claude Code. It is Apache 2.0 for macOS, Windows and Linux, and its last commit landed on 5 Nov 2026. We think it suits Eigent users who want their own agents on the team. See AionUi alternatives.

2. Munder Difflin: a team of agents on your computer or in the cloud #

Munder Difflin runs a team of AI agents on your computer or in the Cloud Office. Its README, read 14 Nov 2026, says it “coordinates a whole office of agents on your own machine”, with agents “coordinated by your clone (Michael)”. Agents run on engines such as Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI, and the README adds “bring-your-own keys and local LLMs”. Work comes in from Slack or a webhook, and agents use MCP servers, skills and plugins. The Cloud Office runs your agents in the cloud, always on, so work goes on with your computer off. A mobile app is coming soon. We think it suits people who want one office of agents for all kinds of knowledge work. Setup is in how to install and use Munder Difflin.

3. Rowboat: agents and people in one group chat #

Rowboat puts your agents and your coworkers in shared group chats. Its README, read 14 Nov 2026, says “Spin up agents, add them to a group chat, and let them collaborate on shared goals. Free and open source.” It runs on the web, iPhone and desktop, and you can self host Harbor, its server. v1.0.9 of the Apache 2.0 repo came out on 11 Nov 2026. We think it suits teams where people and agents share the work. See Rowboat alternatives.

4. Claude Cowork: the paid option from Anthropic #

Claude Cowork is Anthropic’s agent for your files and tools, on a paid Claude plan. Its product page, read 14 Nov 2026, says “Big projects are split into chunks that run together” and “Close your laptop, it keeps going.” Anthropic’s article says Cowork and chat merged into one Claude, with a 12 Nov 2026 update for Claude Team. Claude’s pricing page listed Pro at $20 if billed monthly on 14 Nov 2026. We think it suits people who already pay for Claude. See Claude Cowork alternatives.

5. CrewAI: if you would rather write code #

CrewAI is an open source Python framework for building teams of agents yourself. Its README, read 14 Nov 2026, describes Crews as “Teams of AI agents with true autonomy and agency”. It is MIT, and 1.15.41 came out on 12 Nov 2026. We think it suits developers who want the workforce idea in their own code. See our CrewAI and AutoGen comparison.

How to choose #

Pick by how you want the work split. Our view:

  • AionUi for a leader and teammates in one free desktop app.
  • Munder Difflin for a team of AI agents for all kinds of knowledge work, on your computer or in the Cloud Office.
  • Rowboat if people and agents share one chat.
  • Claude Cowork if you already pay for Claude.
  • CrewAI if you want to build the team in Python.
  • Eigent if you want its workforce, credits and model list in one app.

FAQ

What is Eigent?

Eigent is a desktop app for macOS, Windows and Linux, built on CAMEL-AI's open source project. Its README, read 14 Nov 2026, calls it "the open source Cowork desktop application". Its workforce lets several agents divide one task and work on the parts in parallel.

How much does Eigent cost?

The app is free with your own API keys or local models. On 14 Nov 2026 its pricing page listed Plus at $19.99 a month with 2,000 task credits and Pro at $99.99 a month with 10,000. Teams read "Coming Soon".

Is Eigent open source?

Yes. The eigent-ai/eigent repo on GitHub is Apache 2.0, and v1.0.7 came out on 30 Oct 2026. Its README recommends a local deployment that needs no cloud account.

What is the closest Eigent alternative?

Our view: AionUi, a free Apache 2.0 desktop app whose Team Mode has a leader agent split work across teammates in parallel. Munder Difflin is the pick for a team of AI agents for all kinds of knowledge work, on your computer or in the Cloud Office.

Can Eigent use local models?

Yes. Its README lists local model support through vLLM, Ollama and LM Studio, and its pricing page says the Free plan covers your own keys and local models. Paid plans add task credits and more hosted models.
