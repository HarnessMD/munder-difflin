GTA 6 Characters: Jason, Lucia and the Cast, Explained Without Spoilers

GTA 6 characters without spoilers: Jason Duval, Lucia Caminos and all 8 entries on Rockstar's cast page, each in Rockstar's own words.

CG Chaitanya Giri · Nov 19, 2026 · 5 min read Guides

GTA 6 has two lead characters, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, and Rockstar’s own cast page lists six more: Cal Hampton, Boobie Ike, Dre’Quan Priest, Real Dimez, Raul Bautista and Brian Heder. Checked 19 Nov 2026.

Grand Theft Auto VI launches on 19 Nov 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, per Rockstar’s Newswire. No spoilers: only Rockstar’s pre launch pages and trailer posts, no plot past the premise, nothing from reviews. Everything else on launch week is in our GTA 6 hub.

Who are the main characters in GTA 6? #

Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos are the leads, and Rockstar sells the whole game as their story. The synopsis on Rockstar’s GTA VI page opens: “Jason and Lucia have always known the deck is stacked against them.”

Their profiles stop at the starting line. Jason’s page says he “grew up around grifters and crooks” and landed in the Keys “working for local drug runners.” Lucia’s page says she wants “the good life her mom has dreamed of since their days in Liberty City”.

Every GTA 6 character Rockstar lists, in one table #

Rockstar’s Only in Leonida page has a People list with eight entries, each with its own page. The page’s other half, Places, is in our GTA 6 map guide.

Rockstar’s Launch Trailer post (15 Oct 2026) names Jason and Lucia in its synopsis. Rockstar’s own Trailer 1 post (5 Dec 2023) names nobody, but AP reported that day that the game would “star a female protagonist, a first for the franchise, named Lucia.” Game Informer reported on 6 May 2025 that Trailer 2 “spends a bit more time introducing the audience to Jason Duval”. We found no page tying the other six to a trailer.

How many characters are in GTA 6? #

Rockstar lists 8 entries on its People list; that is our only count. One entry is a duo, per the Real Dimez page: Bae-Luxe and Roxy. So the list names 9 people across 8 entries. Rockstar gives no total for everyone in the game, and we will not guess one.

Other names turn up inside the profiles, such as Brian’s third wife, Lori, and the Ultimate Edition text on Rockstar’s page adds an “eccentric collector and local fixer, Wyman” (more in our editions guide). Rockstar keeps them off the People list, so we do too. Eight pages is a short guest list for an entire state.

How do you read GTA 6 character pages without spoiling yourself? #

Read Rockstar’s own pages first and stop where they stop. We think pre launch copy is the safest read, because it was written to sell the setup.

  1. Start at the People list. Game Informer reported on 6 May 2025 that Rockstar’s site added “new characters”, and the pages end where the story begins.
  2. Read trailer posts, not trailer breakdowns. Rockstar’s posts give the synopsis. Frame by frame breakdowns guess at plot, and some guesses land.
  3. Treat any name outside the eight as a spoiler flag. If a “full cast” list names someone Rockstar’s People list does not, that name came from the game, not the marketing. Close the tab.
  4. Read the score, skip the review. Reviews went live on 18 Nov 2026; save the text for after the credits. Our review scores roundup gives the numbers without the plot.
  5. Skip any video with “ending” in its title. tbreak reports (4 Nov 2026) that under Rockstar’s video policy, “Endings, edited cutscene compilations and standalone cinematics are removed”. Takedowns come after uploads, so titles reach your feed first. Rockstar’s policy page is the one to read.

Voice cast: none of the Rockstar pages we opened credit an actor, so this page names nobody.

Who should you know before you start? #

Know the eight names and how Rockstar’s pages link them; meet everyone else over a long story.

  • Jason’s circle. Cal is “Jason’s friend and a fellow associate of Brian’s”. Brian lets Jason stay at one of his properties in return for help with “local shakedowns”.
  • The music crowd. Boobie is most invested in his partnership with Dre’Quan “for Only Raw Records”, and Real Dimez are “signed to Only Raw Records”.
  • The wild card. “Raul’s recklessness raises the stakes with every score.”
  • Lucia’s side. “Lucia’s committed to her plan”, per her page. What the plan is, the game tells you.
