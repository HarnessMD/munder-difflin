How paying works.

Monthly or yearly
Paid plans are subscriptions, paid through Stripe. They renew each month or each year until you cancel.
Cancel any time
Cancel in Manage billing. You keep access to the end of the period you paid for.
Already paying?
You keep your price.
Refunds
A full refund within 14 days of a plan's first payment. Renewals are not refunded, and the $19 top up is a non refundable recharge.
Tokens
Counted in total: input, output and cache, the number other tools show.
Currency and tax
Prices are in US dollars, and the price is shown before you pay. We may offer lower prices in some countries. Where the law requires tax on a purchase, it may be added.
