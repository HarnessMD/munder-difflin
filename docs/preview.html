What it needs
  1. 1macOS, Windows or Linux. Only the installers that exist for this version are listed; nothing here is promised.
  2. 2At least one supported coding CLI on your path: Claude Code, Codex, Opencode or Antigravity, with your own subscription or key.
  3. 3Nothing else. There is no account for the solo path and there will not be one.
First run
  1. 1Open the app. It finds the CLIs on your path.
  2. 2Give the first agent a task. Watch it on the floor, or press V for the console.
  3. 3Joining a team? Choose Join a team and paste the invite code your admin sent you.

0.5.2

9 September 2026Latest

New

  • Send a capture to the right agent. A screenshot, a spoken message or a meeting transcript can now be aimed at one agent. Before, everything landed on the orchestrator and nothing said so.
  • Find any meeting. Search meetings by name or by the words inside the transcript, filter by date, and rename a meeting so the name means something.
  • Captures clean up after themselves. Screenshots and meeting audio are deleted 24 hours later, automatically. Transcripts are kept.
  • Your name on the team. Set your name in Settings. It travels sealed inside the message, so the relay never sees it.
  • Choose your responder. One setting decides which agent answers a Slack request or a teammate’s message. The orchestrator stays the default.
  • A calmer start. While your floor restores, the app holds a loading screen with a count and a skip instead of drawing a half built office.

Fixed

  • A meeting can no longer record without a transcription key. It refuses up front instead of collecting audio nobody can read.
  • “Send now” on a queued message answers the click: the row moves to the front and a toast says so.
  • Clicking the Dock icon brings the main window back while the voice puck is up.
  • Messages queued for the orchestrator survive a restart.

Plus smaller fixes across the Stapler, the PRO sidebar and the voice tools.
