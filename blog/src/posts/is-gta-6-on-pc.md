No, GTA 6 is not on PC: no PC version has been announced, and Grand Theft Auto VI arrives on 19 Nov 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. Checked 19 Nov 2026.

Rockstar's [launch trailer post](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/517572321a549a/watch-the-grand-theft-auto-vi-launch-trailer) (15 Oct 2026) says: "Grand Theft Auto VI arrives November 19 to PlayStation 5 and XBOX Series X|S." Take-Two's [quarterly results](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reports-results-fiscal-4) (9 Nov 2026) list the same two. Neither mentions PC or rules it out: "not announced", not "never". More launch week guides sit in our [GTA 6 hub](/blog/make-money-with-gta-6/), and online play has its own [GTA 6 Online release date guide](/blog/gta-6-online-release-date/).

## Is there a GTA 6 PC release date or PC requirements?

No. Rockstar has published neither, because there is no announced PC version to attach them to. Any "GTA 6 PC requirements" table online is a guess wearing a spec sheet costume. For GTA V, Rockstar set the PC date and linked the specs in a [24 Feb 2015 Newswire post](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak14o8838472ka/gtav-updates-online-heists-coming-march-10-gtav-for-pc).

GTA V's PC date also slipped. Fake money, real apology. ## What does that history tell us about GTA 6?

Only that Rockstar has launched on console first before. Our view: a later PC version is plausible, but 12 or 19 months is not a forecast, and Rockstar has said nothing.

PC does matter to the publisher. [Insider Gaming reports](https://insider-gaming.com/take-two-ceo-says-console-players-are-core-consumers-for-rockstar/) (4 May 2026), citing Bloomberg, that Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick said: "Now, for a major title, PC sales can account for 45% to 50%." That is a reason to want a PC version, not an announcement of one. Both earlier PC dates came from the [Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire), so a rumour without a Newswire link is just a rumour.

## How can you play GTA 6 if you only have a PC?

You cannot run it on a PC, but a PC can be the screen for a console you own. As of 19 Nov 2026:

1. **Buy a console.** PS5 or Xbox Series X|S ([prices](/blog/gta-6-editions-price/)). Rockstar's [preload post](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10) asks for "up to 212 GB depending on your device" ([download size guide](/blog/gta-6-download-size/)).
2. **Remote Play from that console.** Rockstar's [10 Nov post](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/89834o94713o21/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-now) says "GTAVI will also fully support Remote Play on PlayStation and XBOX." PlayStation's [Remote Play page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/remote-play/) lists Windows PC and Mac, but "A PS4 or PS5 console is required", with "at least 5Mbps" of broadband (15Mbps recommended). The PS5 does the work; your PC just watches.
3. **Cloud gaming: not listed for PC.** GTA VI is not on the [PlayStation Plus games list](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/ps-plus/games/), whose cloud streaming goes "to your PlayStation 5 console or PlayStation Portal remote player". Rockstar puts Xbox Cloud Gaming "on XBOX Series X|S and XBOX One consoles", and the [Xbox Cloud Gaming](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/play) pages we read did not show GTA VI.

## Are GTA 6 PC downloads and emulators safe?

No. There is no PC build, so every "GTA 6 PC download" is malware bait. [Malwarebytes](https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/threat-intel/2026/08/fake-gta-6-extended-look-and-demo-sites-deliver-an-infostealer) (24 Aug 2026) found fake Rockstar sites whose `gta6_installer.exe` was "just 1.1 MB": an infostealer that takes browser passwords, cookies and sessions. Its verdict: "there is no legitimate GTA 6 demo or PC build to download." Also: "the screenshot we took of one of the websites is larger than the file it was offering."

[Help Net Security reports](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/09/10/fake-gta-6-downloads-malware-ransomware/) (10 Sep 2026) that Huntress took apart a fake "leaked" ISO with "several RATs, an infostealer, ransomware used as a wiper". [LevelUp reports](https://www.levelup.com/en/news/scammers-are-using-fake-ps5-emulators-to-trick-gamers-and-steal-their-private-information-with-malware/) (30 Oct 2026) NordVPN's warning about fake PS5 emulators on GitHub: "But this isn’t an emulator. It steals your data, hides from your antivirus, and erases its tracks".

- Buy only from PlayStation Store, the Xbox store or a real retailer.
- Treat any GTA 6 PC, crack, demo or emulator link as hostile.
- Ran one? Malwarebytes says to scan the PC, change passwords from a clean device (email first), sign out of active sessions everywhere and turn on two factor authentication. Our advice: with RATs and ransomware in these bundles, reset the PC too.

## Why do studios ship on PC later?

To serve their core players first on fixed hardware, then do the harder PC work. Insider Gaming reports that Zelnick told Bloomberg Rockstar will be "judged by serving the core", and the core consumer must be "served first and best". Asked about a PlayStation marketing deal, he said: "No, historically, Rockstar has released games on console first."

Our view: a console is a short list of known machines; a PC port is an open ended test matrix. Rockstar promised GTA V on PC would be "fully optimized to support a broad range of current hardware", and its launch post listed "over 25 separate configurable settings" plus triple monitor support. Each is something to build, test and support.
