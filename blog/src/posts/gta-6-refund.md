On PlayStation, a GTA 6 refund needs an untouched download (preloading counts) and a request within 14 days of paying, while Xbox typically refunds requests made within 14 days if you have little play time, and either way the store decides. Checked 19 Nov 2026.

Grand Theft Auto VI arrived on 19 November on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, per [Rockstar](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/517572321a549a/watch-the-grand-theft-auto-vi-launch-trailer). For the rest of the launch, start at [our GTA 6 hub](/blog/make-money-with-gta-6/).

## Can you get a refund for GTA 6?

Sometimes, and never automatically. Sony's [PlayStation Store cancellation policy](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/legal/playstation-store-cancellation-policy/) refunds a game "within 14 days from the date of purchase", "provided that you have not started downloading or streaming it." Microsoft starts from the other end. Its [Digital Game Purchase Refund Terms](https://support.xbox.com/en-US/help/subscriptions-billing/buy-games-apps/digital-game-purchase-refund-terms) call these sales "final, but we understand there may be extenuating circumstances", and its [store returns page](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/store/b/returns) says digital games "are not eligible for a return unless applicable law states that you're eligible for a refund."

Sony says you "may have additional rights under applicable local law", and its [UK](https://www.playstation.com/en-gb/legal/playstation-store-cancellation-policy/) and [India](https://www.playstation.com/en-in/legal/playstation-store-cancellation-policy/) policies match the US one on games and preorders.

## Does preloading GTA 6 block a PS5 refund?

Yes. Sony's preorder rule holds only if you have not started downloading the main part, "including pre-loading before release". PS5 preloading opened on 10 Nov at midnight local time, per [Rockstar](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10), and Sony warns that "downloads will happen automatically if Automatic Downloads are enabled". Your console may have spent your refund at midnight while you slept. It was trying to help.

If nothing has downloaded, the day you paid sets the window (our arithmetic for a 19 Nov release):

- **Paid on or before 4 Nov:** only "before the release date", which has passed.
- **Paid from 5 Nov:** "up to 14 days after payment was taken". Paid 15 Nov? Ask by 29 Nov.
- **Bought on launch day:** 14 days, so until about 3 Dec.

Cancel, and any preorder bonus "will stop working".

## How do you request a GTA 6 refund on PS5?

You ask from Transaction History, and Sony decides. From Sony's [refund request page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/store/ps-store-refund-request/) and [transaction history guide](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/store/check-ps-store-transaction-subscription-service/):

1. **Check eligibility:** "Content has not been downloaded or streamed", and you ask "within 14 days of the purchase."
2. **Nothing downloaded?** Turn off Automatic Downloads first.
3. **Open Transaction History:** Account Management on the web, or on PS5 Settings > Users and Accounts > Account > Payment and Subscriptions > Transaction History.
4. **Select GTA VI, then Request Refund** (Cancel Purchase on the UK page), and "follow the on-screen instructions."
5. **No button?** Contact PlayStation Support with your Online ID, Sign-In ID and the purchase name. For a preorder, Sony's [preorder page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/store/cancel-ps-store-pre-order/) says "To cancel your pre-order, please contact us."

## How do you request a GTA 6 refund on Xbox?

Sign in on Microsoft's refund page and pick the game. The [page](https://support.xbox.com/en-US/help/subscriptions-billing/buy-games-apps/refund-orders) reads the same in the US, UK and India: refunds are "typically issued for requests made within 14 days of the game's purchase date", and "not all requests will be accepted."

The preorder exit closed on 9 Nov (our arithmetic): [Order history](https://account.microsoft.com/billing/orders/) cancels "up to 10 days before the game launches". After that, the [preorder FAQ](https://support.xbox.com/en-US/help/subscriptions-billing/buy-games-apps/pre-order-faq) says "to cancel you'll need to request a refund."

1. **Sign in** on [Request a refund for digital games](https://support.xbox.com/en-US/help/subscriptions-billing/buy-games-apps/refund-orders) with the account that paid.
2. **Pick GTA VI.** Missing? See [I can't find a purchase in the refund request list](https://support.xbox.com/en-US/help/subscriptions-billing/buy-games-apps/why-dont-i-see-my-purchase).
3. **Say why.** The terms ask: "Tell us why you're requesting a refund."
4. **Wait for the email.** Meanwhile "you'll no longer be able to access the digital game product."
5. **Denied?** Appeal "by replying to the email message". You "cannot resubmit the same refund request".

## What about a boxed copy?

Ask the shop that sold it; its own returns policy governs. Sony's [GTA VI support page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/games/gta-vi-support/) says the box holds "a game download code" and "A disc will not be included in the box." Sony's policy "does not cover refunds for purchases made outside of PlayStation™Store", and its refund page sends code buyers to "the retailer that sold the voucher code to you". Our view: read the shop's returns page before you redeem the code.

## How long does a GTA 6 refund take?

Weeks on a US PlayStation account, days elsewhere. Sony's US refund page says "30 to 60 days"; its [UK](https://www.playstation.com/en-gb/support/store/ps-store-refund-request/) and [India](https://www.playstation.com/en-in/support/store/ps-store-refund-request/) pages say "10 days". All three pay your original payment method "Where possible", else your wallet. Microsoft: "several business days or longer".
