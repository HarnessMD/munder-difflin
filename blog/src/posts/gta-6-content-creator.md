## How do you capture GTA 6 clips on PS5?

Press the create button. [Sony's capture page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/games/capture-ps5-gameplay-screenshots/), read 19 Nov 2026, says Save Recent Gameplay keeps "anywhere from the last 15 seconds up to 1 hour of gameplay" and a new recording runs "up to 1 hour". Resolution sits in Settings > Captures and Broadcasts > Captures > Video Clip Format. Note: "Some scenes are blocked and won't be included in your video clips."

To export, open Media Gallery, pick a clip, then More > Copy to USB Drive ([Sony](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/games/ps5-media-gallery-view-screenshots/)). The PlayStation App only takes clips "up to 1920x1080 in resolution and up to 3 minutes in duration" and keeps them 14 days ([Sony](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/games/ps5-game-captures-ps-app/)). GTA 6 clips for edits in 4K go by USB; the app stops at 1920x1080.

## How do you capture GTA 6 clips on Xbox Series X|S?

Press the Xbox button, then X, to save the last 30 seconds. [Microsoft's capture page](https://support.xbox.com/en-US/help/friends-social-activity/share-socialize/capture-game-clips-and-screenshots), read 19 Nov 2026, says the View button grabs "up to the last 2 minutes", and Start recording runs up to 10 minutes on internal storage or 1 hour on external, depending on clip resolution and free space. Resolution lives in Profile & system > Settings > Preferences > Capture & share. For some games it is fixed at 4K HDR, and then internal clips stop at 30 seconds ([Microsoft](https://support.xbox.com/en-US/help/games-apps/game-setup-and-play/game-clips-captured-only-4K-HDR)).

To export, open the Captures app, select Manage, then Upload to OneDrive or Copy to external storage; the drive "must be formatted as NTFS" ([Microsoft](https://support.xbox.com/en-US/help/games-apps/my-games-apps/back-up-game-clips)). Back up early: clips on the Xbox network "may be deleted after 90 days".

| Step (checked 19 Nov 2026) | PS5 | Xbox |
|---|---|---|
| Save what just happened | 15 seconds to 1 hour | 30 seconds, or up to 2 minutes |
| New recording | Up to 1 hour | 10 minutes internal, 1 hour external |
| Export | Copy to USB Drive | OneDrive, or an NTFS drive |

## What does Rockstar allow you to post?

Gameplay with context, reportedly, and no story. [tbreak](https://tbreak.com/gta-6-spoilers-rockstar-video-takedown-policy/) and [Metro](https://metro.co.uk/2026/11/04/rockstar-will-take-videos-gta-6-story-spoilers-even-launch-29765723/) reported on 4 Nov 2026 that Rockstar removes endings, edited cutscene compilations, isolated cinematics and footage from before release, while cutscenes inside a longer, narrated playthrough stay. tbreak reports the policy page frames permission "around non-commercial use", so read [Rockstar's policy page](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/200153756/Policy-on-posting-copyrighted-Rockstar-Games-material) before you earn from footage. This is not legal advice.

Our view, as three creator rules: every Short is gameplay, never a cutscene; every thumbnail is your own capture of play, never a story moment; nothing shows a mod, since Metro reports footage "promoting modifications/ports" is targeted too.

## What size is a GTA 6 YouTube thumbnail and banner?

YouTube recommends 3840 x 2160 for video thumbnails and 2160 x 3840 for Shorts. [YouTube's thumbnail page](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431), read 19 Nov 2026, also suggests a format such as JPG or PNG, 16:9 for videos and 9:16 for Shorts, and caps files at 50MB from a computer or 2 MB from mobile. Custom thumbnails need a verified account.

[YouTube's branding page](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525) sets a minimum of 2048 x 1152 at 16:9 and a 6 MB cap; at that size, text and logos go inside a 1235 x 338 safe area, because the image "will get cropped on certain views and devices".

For vertical video, [YouTube's Shorts page](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12779649) takes files "Up to 3 minutes" with "a square or vertical aspect ratio". For a GTA 6 TikTok account, check TikTok's Help Center for its limits.

## How do you write GTA 6 titles without spoilers?

Name what you did, not what happened in the story. Our rules:

- Lead with the activity: a drive, a stunt, a place, a fail.
- No character fates, no mission names, no "ending" anywhere, tags included.
- Write "no spoilers" only when it is true for every frame.
- Thumbnails too. A frozen cutscene frame is a spoiler with a border.
- Keep a spoiler word list for the title agent.

Do this: "Two hours of Vice City driving (no spoilers)". Not that: a title asking what happens to a character, which the comments will answer for everyone.

## What does the agent team prepare while you play?

Five agents prepare; you approve. While you play and record:

1. **Cutter** trims the moments you marked with ffmpeg and makes a vertical cut.
2. **Captioner** turns a transcript into subtitles you correct.
3. **Title writer** drafts titles and checks them against your spoiler list.
4. **Scheduler** drafts a posting calendar. We think one long video and a few Shorts a week is plenty at launch.
5. **Tracker** keeps one sheet: clip, platform, date, link and the views you paste in.
6. **You** watch every cut, approve it and post from your own account.

```
ffmpeg -ss 00:12:40 -i capture.mp4 -t 30 -vf "crop=ih*9/16:ih" short_01.mp4
ffmpeg -i short_01.mp4 -vf "subtitles=short_01.srt" short_01_captioned.mp4
```

The first line cuts 30 seconds from 12:40 into a 9:16 slice, centred because [ffmpeg's docs](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html) say the crop's left edge "defaults to" `(in_w-out_w)/2`. The second burns in captions. A coding agent such as Claude Code can run both; that is ffmpeg's work, not a Munder Difflin feature.
