GTA 6 takes about 80 hours to beat, going by the best sourced figure: Rockstar North's Rob Nelson said his own playthrough, the main story plus some optional goals, took 80 hours, [TechRadar reports](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/gta-6-will-be-a-massive-game-and-rockstars-longest-yet-as-the-studio-confirms-it-can-take-up-to-80-hours-to-beat). That is one developer's run, not an average. Checked 19 Nov 2026.

Grand Theft Auto VI launches on 19 Nov 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, per [Rockstar's Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/517572321a549a/watch-the-grand-theft-auto-vi-launch-trailer). Reviewers who give a number finished in 70 to almost 120 hours, and HowLongToBeat holds 6 submitted times so far. Below: every figure, its source, and what it means if you have a job. For the rest of launch week, start at [our GTA 6 hub](/blog/make-money-with-gta-6/).

## Where does the 80 hour figure come from?

It comes from Rob Nelson, head of development at Rockstar North, in an interview with The New York Times that TechRadar covered on 28 Aug 2026. TechRadar's words: "his playthrough took 80 hours to finish, including the main story and some 'optional goals with narrative ramifications.'"

Three caveats. It is one run. It includes optional content, so it is not a pure main story time. And the player helped make the game. Our view: that is like timing a maze by asking the person who drew it.

We think a playtime quote in a newspaper three months before launch is also marketing; more in [GTA 6 marketing lessons](/blog/gta-6-marketing/).

## How long did reviewers take to finish GTA 6?

Between 70 hours and almost 120 hours, in the reviews that state a number. Reviews went live on 18 Nov 2026:

- **The Guardian**, Keza MacDonald: "[It took me 70 hours to finish the game and I could have taken longer.](https://www.theguardian.com/games/2026/nov/18/grand-theft-auto-vi-gta-6-review-playstation-rockstar)"
- **IGN**, Luke Reilly: "[despite hitting the credits after almost 120 hours of hijinks](https://www.ign.com/articles/grand-theft-auto-vi-review)". His verdict adds: "After three weeks and over 120 hours playing it", and he has "zero plans to stop playing it".
- **CGMagazine**, as quoted on [Metacritic](https://www.metacritic.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi/critic-reviews/): the story kept the reviewer "sitting on the edge of my seat for over seventy hours."
- **Generación Xbox**, as quoted on [Metacritic](https://www.metacritic.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi/critic-reviews/?platform=xbox-series-x): "After more than 80 hours on Xbox Series X".

Eurogamer, Polygon and Game Informer give no total. For scores instead of hours, see [GTA 6 review scores](/blog/gta-6-review-scores/).

## What does HowLongToBeat say about GTA 6?

[HowLongToBeat lists GTA VI](https://howlongtobeat.com/game/104609) with 6 submitted times, read early on 19 Nov 2026, which is too few to trust yet:

- **Main Story:** 42 hours, from 1 submission: a sample size of 1.
- **Main + Sides:** 80 hours, from 5 submissions (Rushed 70 hours, Leisure 95 hours).
- **Completionist:** no times yet.

Six people is a sample, not a statistic. Check again in a few weeks.

## Is GTA 6 longer than GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2?

We think it is longer than GTA 5 and close to Red Dead Redemption 2, but the GTA 6 side rests on 6 early times. HowLongToBeat, read 19 Nov 2026:

- **GTA V** ([page](https://howlongtobeat.com/game/4064)): Main Story 32 hours from 3.4K submitted times, Main + Sides 51 hours, Completionist 89½ hours.
- **Red Dead Redemption 2** ([page](https://howlongtobeat.com/game/27100)): Main Story 50½ hours from 2.5K times, Main + Sides 84½ hours, Completionist 196 hours.
- **GTA VI**: Main Story 42 hours from 1 time, Main + Sides 80 hours from 5, Completionist none yet.

Compare like with like. Nelson's 80 hours includes optional goals, so set it next to Main + Sides: 51 hours for GTA V, 84½ for Red Dead Redemption 2.

## How many weeks is that with a full time job?

At 2 hours a weeknight, 80 hours takes 8 weeks. Plain arithmetic, Monday to Friday, starting Thursday 19 Nov 2026, no nights off:

- **1 hour a weeknight:** 5 hours a week, 16 weeks. Credits around Wednesday 10 Mar 2027.
- **2 hours a weeknight:** 10 hours a week, 8 weeks. Credits around Wednesday 13 Jan 2027.
- **3 hours a weeknight:** 15 hours a week, a little over 5 weeks. Credits on Friday 25 Dec 2026.

Every night instead: 7, 14 or 21 hours a week, so about 11, 6 or 4 weeks. At IGN's almost 120 hours, 2 hours a weeknight is 12 weeks. At 3 hours a weeknight, the credits roll on Christmas Day, which is either perfect timing or a family conversation.
