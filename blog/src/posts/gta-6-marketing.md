GTA 6 marketing ran on scarcity and dates: two trailers 17 months apart, two delay notes that each named an exact day, and a launch date that held for 378 days until Grand Theft Auto VI arrived on 19 Nov 2026. Checked 19 Nov 2026.

Take-Two's [9 Nov 2026 results](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reports-results-fiscal-4) put preorders at "unprecedented levels". More launch angles live in [our GTA 6 hub](/blog/make-money-with-gta-6/).

## What did the GTA 6 marketing timeline look like?

It ran just over four years, from a 2022 leak statement to a 2026 Miami festival.

| Date | Move | What it did |
|---|---|---|
| 19 Sep 2022 | Leak confirmed | Reveal promised "when it is ready" |
| 8 Nov 2023 | First trailer promised for early December | A month to wait |
| 4 Dec 2023 | Trailer 1, early, after a leak | 90,421,491 YouTube views in 24 hours, Guinness reported on 6 Dec 2023 |
| 2 May 2025 | Delay to 26 May 2026 | Investors told the same day |
| 6 May 2025 | Trailer 2 and a website update | 475 million views in 24 hours, Rockstar says, TechRadar reported on 8 May 2025 |
| 6 Nov 2025 | Delay to 19 Nov 2026 | Date never moved again |
| 25 Jun 2026 | Preorders open | 7.71 million copies by 3 Nov, a Sensor Tower estimate [Metro](https://metro.co.uk/2026/11/04/rockstar-will-take-videos-gta-6-story-spoilers-even-launch-29765723/) reported on 4 Nov 2026 |
| 3 Nov 2026 | Preload moved to 10 Nov | Two days earlier |
| 9 Nov 2026 | Miami festival announced | A launch with a venue |

## How much did GTA 6 marketing cost?

No figure is published. As of 19 Nov 2026, Take-Two reports selling and marketing only as one line for the whole company, across every game it sells, so its results give no GTA VI number. No outlet we read names a sourced GTA VI budget, so any number you see is unsourced.

## 1. Answer a leak in one paragraph

Confirm it, say what stays the same, promise the real reveal. On 19 Sep 2022 Rockstar said it was "extremely disappointed" by the leak and would "properly introduce you to this next game when it is ready", [The Record reported](https://therecord.media/rockstar-confirms-cyberattack-leak-of-confidential-data-including-gta-6-footage). No new footage, no date, no argument. The leak was already the story; the statement did not write a sequel.

## 2. Announce the announcement

A date for the reveal turns waiting into an event. On 8 Nov 2023 a note signed by Rockstar cofounder Sam Houser promised the first trailer "in early December" ([MobileSyrup](https://mobilesyrup.com/2023/11/08/after-10-years-rockstar-will-finally-unveil-gta-vi-in-december/)). When it leaked, Rockstar posted it "roughly 15 hours before the planned Tuesday morning unveiling", the [AP reported](https://www.news4jax.com/business/2023/12/05/grand-theft-auto-vi-leak-followed-by-an-official-trailer-with-a-twist-a-release-date-of-2025/) on 5 Dec 2023. The answer to a leaked trailer was the real one: [Guinness](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/news/2023/12/grand-theft-auto-vi-first-look-becomes-most-viewed-videogame-trailer-in-24-hours-762030) reported 90,421,491 YouTube views in 24 hours on 6 Dec 2023.

## 3. Make each trailer rare and complete

We think scarcity works only when each drop is big. Rockstar's posting schedule for trailers was simple: rarely, and complete each time. Trailer 2 came on 6 May 2025, 17 months after Trailer 1, with "screenshots, new characters, and more" added to the website ([Game Informer](https://www.gameinformer.com/2025/05/06/rockstar-just-unveiled-the-second-grand-theft-auto-vi-trailer)). Rockstar says it drew 475 million views across all platforms in 24 hours ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/the-latest-grand-theft-auto-6-trailer-has-set-a-major-record-by-racking-up-475-million-views-in-just-24-hours), 8 May 2025, citing The Hollywood Reporter): a company figure, not an audit.

## 4. Say sorry in your own words, with a date

Both delay notes apologised plainly, gave an exact new date and one reason, and said thank you. Nobody is blamed. [2 May 2025](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/258aa538o412ok/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-now-coming-may-26-2026): "We are very sorry that this is later than you expected." [6 Nov 2025](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak3ak31a49a221/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-now-set-to-launch-november-19-2026): "We are sorry for adding additional time to what we realize has been a long wait", with a weekday, "Thursday, November 19, 2026". The first runs seven sentences, the second four. Practice helps. Four days after the first, Trailer 2 arrived: bad news, then something to watch.

## 5. Tell investors and players on the same day

Each delay reached players and shareholders on the same day. On 2 May 2025 Take-Two "reiterated its expectations" of "record levels of" net bookings in [its release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reiterates-expectations); the second delay sat inside its [6 Nov 2025 results](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reports-results-fiscal-3). CEO Strauss Zelnick told [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-still-needs-marketing-even-if-everyones-heard-of-it-take-two-boss-says-take-awareness-and-turn-it-into-excitement/) on 17 Sep 2026 why a famous game still needs marketing: "awareness is one thing; enthusiasm is another thing."

## 6. Let players and creators carry the story

Give them rules and free assets, then step aside. At 12:30 UTC on 18 Nov 2026, before the game opened in the UAE, 6.38 million people watched GTA 6 streams at once on Twitch, YouTube and Kick, with no viewer rewards attached, [tbreak reported](https://tbreak.com/gta-6-streaming-record-before-launch/) that day, citing Eurogamer and preliminary Streams Charts data. Creators got public rules: [tbreak](https://tbreak.com/gta-6-spoilers-rockstar-video-takedown-policy/) and Metro reported on 4 Nov 2026 that prerelease footage comes down and cutscenes inside longer playthroughs stay. Read [Rockstar's policy page](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/200153756/Policy-on-posting-copyrighted-Rockstar-Games-material), or our [GTA 6 streaming setup](/blog/gta-6-streaming-setup/). Rockstar also promised a free printable poster and map for launch day ([3 Nov 2026](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10)).

Miami gets a party on 21 Nov at Mana Wynwood, "Presented in partnership with PlayStation" ([Rockstar, 9 Nov 2026](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/o39o87a435a25a/grand-theft-auto-vi-the-festival); [The FADER](https://www.thefader.com/2026/11/09/gta-vi-festival-miami-lineup-tickets) reported it too).

## 7. Put one date on the front page

Lead with one date and keep it. On 19 Nov 2026 Rockstar's [GTA VI page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI) still opened on "Coming November 19, 2026". It took two moves: "Coming 2025" at the [reveal](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/8978kok9385a82/grand-theft-auto-vi-watch-trailer-1-now), then 26 May 2026, then the date set on 6 Nov 2025. Changes since: none, for 378 days. A later change went the buyer's way: preload moved from 12 Nov ([24 Jun 2026 post](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/5171972o3ak5oa/pre-order-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-june-25)) to 10 Nov. We think a date that moves earlier is the rarest sentence in launch planning.

## What can a small team copy this week?

Five habits that cost nothing but discipline:

1. **One date page.** Change it only to move closer.
2. **A delay note template.** Sorry, exact date, one reason, thanks.
3. **A date for the reveal.** Name the demo day, then hit it.
4. **One fact sheet.** Users and investors hear news the same day.
5. **Creator rules.** What is fine to post, plus free files.

## What can't a small team copy?

The silence. Our view: Rockstar could go 17 months between trailers because millions kept waiting; a startup quiet for 17 months is just quiet. Nor the reach. Copy the discipline, not the scale.
