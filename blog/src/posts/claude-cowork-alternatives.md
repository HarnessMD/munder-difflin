The best Claude Cowork alternatives are Hob if you want a free app with the model on your own computer, [Munder Difflin](https://harnessmd.com/download) if you want a team of agents, and ChatGPT Work if you want a big vendor's cloud. Goose, OpenClaw and Hermes Agent round out the list.

[Munder Difflin](https://harnessmd.com/download) is free and open source: a desktop app that runs a team of AI agents for all kinds of knowledge work, coding included, on your own computer. New to Cowork? Start with [What is Claude Cowork?](/blog/what-is-claude-cowork/). Every fact below comes from pages opened on 27 Oct 2026.

## Why look for a Claude Cowork alternative?

Cowork is good at office work: you describe an outcome and Claude hands back finished documents, organized files or research. Three things send people looking elsewhere. From Anthropic's [help page](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork) and [pricing page](https://claude.com/pricing), read 27 Oct 2026:

- **It needs a paid plan.** "Cowork is available to paid Claude plans (Pro, Max, Team, Enterprise) only." Pro is $20 a month billed monthly, or $17 a month billed annually.
- **The work runs in Anthropic's cloud.** "Claude's work runs on Anthropic's servers, in an isolated environment, and your sessions and files are saved to your Claude account." Local files and your browser need the Claude Desktop app open and connected.
- **It is one Claude.** The pricing page says "Claude Cowork is now just Claude": chat and Cowork are becoming one experience, rolling out to Pro and Max first. Claude can split a task across sub-agents, all on Claude models.

## What are the best Claude Cowork alternatives?

Six agents that take a task and hand back finished work, five of them free and open source.

1. **[Hob](https://github.com/FrigadeHQ/hob).** A free, MIT licensed desktop app for Mac, Windows and Linux. Its README calls it "A free, open-source alternative to Claude and ChatGPT". Its Show HN post says it picks an open weight model that fits your computer (Gemma 4 or gpt-oss today), reads and changes files in a folder, runs Python in a sandbox, looks at images, searches the web and takes MCP connectors. v0.2.1 shipped on 26 Oct, and its Show HN post had more than 120 points on 27 Oct.
2. **[Munder Difflin](https://harnessmd.com/download).** A team of AI agents on your own computer, each with a role, for research, writing, email, analysis, ops and code. Agents run on engines such as Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI, take MCP servers, skills and plugins, and connect through Slack and webhooks. Work can run on schedules. The Cloud Office, coming soon, keeps agents working with your computer off.
3. **[ChatGPT Work](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/get-started-with-work).** OpenAI's answer to Cowork: "ChatGPT Work is a way to delegate real work to ChatGPT." It uses your files, plugins and approved tools to create finished files you review, and in the desktop app it can also use local files, apps and the browser.
4. **[Goose](https://github.com/aaif-goose/goose).** "a general-purpose AI agent that runs on your machine," with a desktop app for macOS, Linux and Windows, a CLI and an API. Its README names research, writing, automation and data analysis, not just code. Apache 2.0, part of the Agentic AI Foundation at the Linux Foundation, v1.56.0 on 23 Oct.
5. **[OpenClaw](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw).** An open source assistant that runs on your own computer and answers in the chat apps you already use: Discord, iMessage, Slack, Teams, Telegram, WhatsApp and 20 or more others. MIT, v2026.10.7 on 24 Oct. Its README says it has "no paid tier, hosted service, or token".
6. **[Hermes Agent](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent).** Nous Research's agent that writes skills from experience and keeps memory across sessions. It can live on a small server and take work from Telegram, Discord, Slack, WhatsApp or Signal, with a built in scheduler. MIT, v0.22.5 on 23 Oct.

## Where does the work run?

This is the biggest split. Cowork runs tasks in Anthropic's cloud; ChatGPT Work can run them in OpenAI's cloud or locally through its desktop app. The open source picks run on hardware you control.

- **On your computer, model included:** Hob. Its author says the only parts that go online are web search, which can be turned off in settings, and the MCP connectors you add. Its docs add that it also goes online to download models and, with web access on, to install Python packages.
- **On your computer, with a model you choose:** Goose, Munder Difflin and OpenClaw. Goose's README lists 15 or more providers, Ollama for local models among them, and says it can use an existing Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini subscription.
- **On a server you rent:** Hermes Agent's README, read 27 Oct 2026, says it runs "on a $5 VPS, a GPU cluster, or serverless infrastructure".
- **In the vendor's cloud:** Claude Cowork, and ChatGPT Work when you choose cloud Work; both reach your computer through their desktop apps.

The trade is plain. Cloud tasks keep going when your laptop closes. Local tasks keep your files on hardware you control, though a hosted model still sees what you send it, and they stop when that machine does.

## Which Claude Cowork alternative should you pick?

Start from what you want to hand off:

- **One task at a time, no subscription, files on your machine:** Hob.
- **A team of agents for research, writing, ops and code, on your own computer:** Munder Difflin. See the [install guide](/blog/how-to-install-and-use-munder-difflin/).
- **Cowork's experience on OpenAI's models:** ChatGPT Work.
- **One open agent that works with any provider:** Goose.
- **An assistant you message from your chat apps:** OpenClaw. More picks in [OpenClaw alternatives](/blog/openclaw-alternatives/).
- **An agent that learns and lives on a server:** Hermes Agent. More picks in [Hermes Agent alternatives](/blog/hermes-agent-alternatives/).
