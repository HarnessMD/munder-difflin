GTA 6 comes in two editions, priced alike on PlayStation Store and the Xbox store for the 19 Nov 2026 launch: Standard at $79.99, £69.99, €79.99 or Rs 5,999, and Ultimate at $99.99, £89.99, €99.99 or Rs 7,499. Checked 19 Nov 2026.

Grand Theft Auto VI "arrives November 19 to PlayStation 5 and XBOX Series X|S", per [Rockstar](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/517572321a549a/watch-the-grand-theft-auto-vi-launch-trailer) (15 Oct 2026). ## What are the GTA 6 editions?

There are two editions, Standard and Ultimate, plus an extra for Standard owners: the Ultimate Edition Upgrade. Both editions contain the same game, which is the most useful sentence in this post. The [PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/) and [Xbox](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/games/store/grand-theft-auto-vi/9P3H4968GRSM) store pages, read 19 Nov 2026, list:

- **Standard Edition:** the game, the Vintage Vice City Pack, and one month of GTA+ for digital buyers before the offer closes.
- **Ultimate Edition:** Standard plus the Ultimate Edition Upgrade.
- **Ultimate Edition Upgrade:** the extras alone. Xbox sells it; PlayStation shows no price.

Rockstar's [GTA VI page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI) describes the Vintage Vice City Pack as a "two-tone '55 Vapid Stanier sedan and garage" plus "outfits and hairstyles for both Jason and Lucia". The upgrade, per PlayStation's US page, adds vehicles such as the '95 Grotti Cheetah, outfits, a classic car commission from Wyman, and five places "Only open for business in the Ultimate Edition": Rideout Customs, Sara's Unisex Salon, Stock 305, Electric Fang Tattoo and One-Eyed Willie's.

## Is the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition worth it?

Our view: only if you want the extra cars, outfits and the five Ultimate only places, because the game itself is identical. On Xbox the decision can wait: on 19 Nov 2026 the Ultimate Edition Upgrade cost $20.00, exactly the gap between $79.99 and $99.99, and the same held in pounds, euros and rupees (Rs 5,999 plus Rs 1,500 is Rs 7,499). PlayStation's pages show "Unavailable" where the upgrade's price would be.

On Xbox, twenty dollars gets you a buggy, a boat and a tattoo parlour's opening hours, the most Leonida receipt possible. Our view, by buyer:

- **Story first:** Standard. Same game, less money.
- **Garages and salons:** Ultimate, for the five places that open only there.
- **Unsure, on Xbox:** Standard now, the upgrade later; at 19 Nov 2026 prices the total is the same.
- **Unsure, on PlayStation:** decide before you pay, since no upgrade price is shown.

## What do the store pages say about offers, taxes and regions?

They spell out a launch offer and its limits, though none of the eight pages read on 19 Nov 2026 shows a tax line.

- **Launch bonus:** digital buyers get the Vintage Vice City Pack and one month of GTA+ if they buy "by November 19, 2026" on PlayStation, or "prior to 19 November 2026 23:59:59" on Xbox.
- **GTA+ renews:** "After one month, your membership will continue monthly at the regular monthly price until canceled," says PlayStation, where the month must be claimed by March 31, 2027. Xbox: "must be redeemed within 180 days after placing your pre-order".
- **No bonus with the upgrade:** "Ultimate Edition Upgrade is not eligible for this offer," says PlayStation.
- **Countries left out:** PlayStation's GTA+ offer covers "All PlayStation Country Stores, excluding Bahrain, China, Kuwait, Lebanon, Oman, Qatar, Russian Federation, Taiwan".
- **The Xbox plus sign:** prices such as "$79.99+" carry a plus on 19 Nov 2026, and the same page notes "+Offers in-app purchases".

Our view: set a reminder before the GTA+ month renews. Bought the wrong edition? The [GTA 6 refund guide](/blog/gta-6-refund/) covers both stores.

## How do I check the GTA 6 price myself?

Open each store's page for your country and read three things: edition prices, the upgrade row and the offer deadline.

1. **PlayStation:** open `playstation.com/en-in/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/`, swap `en-in` for your locale (`en-us`, `en-gb`, `en-ie`) and scroll to "Editions".
2. **Xbox:** open `xbox.com/en-IN/games/store/grand-theft-auto-vi/9P3H4968GRSM` with your locale, scroll to "Compare editions", and find the upgrade under "Add-ons for this game".
3. **The offer:** read PlayStation's "GRAND THEFT AUTO VI AND GTA+ OFFER" block, or the Xbox "Description".
4. **Write down the date.** Prices move, and a launch week screenshot is a museum piece by spring.
