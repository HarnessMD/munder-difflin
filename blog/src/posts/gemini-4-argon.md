## What is Gemini 4 Argon?

Gemini 4 Argon is Google's newest top model, built for long jobs in coding, office work and security. Google's [developer post](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/build-with-gemini-4-argon/) of 13 October 2026 calls it "our frontier model for complex workflows across real-world software engineering, enterprise knowledge work, and cybersecurity defense".

Google's posts do not say why it is called Argon. The element is a noble gas that reacts with almost nothing, which is not the sales pitch here.

The rows come from Google's [model card](https://deepmind.google/models/model-cards/gemini-4-argon/) and [models page](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/models), read on 13 Oct 2026.

| | Gemini 4 Argon |
| --- | --- |
| API model ID | gemini-4-argon |
| Input | Text, images, audio and video |
| Output | Text |
| Context window | Up to 1M tokens, input and output combined |
| Knowledge cutoff | March 2026 |

It is the first of a set. Google's [launch post](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-argon/) says "We're looking forward to bringing you the rest of the Gemini 4 family", with no names and no dates. The [Hacker News thread](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50073879) had more than 400 points on 13 Oct 2026.

## When was Gemini 4 Argon released?

Gemini 4 Argon went on general release on 13 October 2026. Google's launch post, dated Oct 13, 2026, says the model "is now globally available today for developers and enterprises".

It was not a surprise. [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1002980/google-gemini-4-argon) reported on 30 September 2026 that Google had revealed the model and was holding it for "trusted cyber defenders" at first. The curtain opened in two pulls.

## Where can you use Gemini 4 Argon?

You can use it through the paid Gemini API, in Google AI Studio and Antigravity, and in the Gemini app, depending on what you pay for. The rows are our summary of Google's launch post and its [Antigravity post](https://antigravity.google/blog/gemini-4-argon-in-google-antigravity), both dated 13 Oct 2026.

| Where | Who gets it |
| --- | --- |
| Gemini API | Paid tier |
| Google AI Studio | Paid tier, or Google AI Ultra subscribers |
| Android Studio | Paid tier of the Gemini API |
| Antigravity desktop app and CLI | Google AI Ultra subscribers |
| Gemini app and AI Mode in Search | Google AI Ultra and Pro subscribers |

The free door is shut. Google's [pricing page](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing) says Gemini 4 Argon "is not available on the Free tier or on the Google AI Pro plan". That note sits on the API pricing page; the launch post still lists Pro subscribers for the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search, and Google's [plans page](https://one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans/) says it is "rolling out to Google AI Pro subscribers in select countries". For businesses, the Antigravity post lists the API in Gemini platform and Antigravity "on eligible Gemini Enterprise subscriptions".

## What does Gemini 4 Argon cost?

Gemini 4 Argon costs $2.00 input and $10.00 output per 1M tokens through 30 November 2026, then $4.00 and $20.00 from 1 December 2026, on Google's pricing page as read on 13 Oct 2026. The output price includes thinking tokens.

| Paid tier, per 1M tokens | Through 30 Nov 2026 | From 1 Dec 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Input | $2.00 | $4.00 |
| Output | $10.00 | $20.00 |
| Context caching | $0.10 | $0.20 |

Introductory prices are the free trial of the API world: pleasant until the calendar turns. Budget on the December column. The pricing page lists batch prices at half of each figure.

On Google's own table, Gemini 4 Argon has the top score in 13 of 19 rows, shares one and trails in five. The four coding rows split two and two. These are Google's figures from its [Gemini 4 Argon page](https://deepmind.google/models/gemini/), read on 13 Oct 2026.

## How does Google say Gemini 4 Argon behaves as an agent?

Google reports fewer shortcuts and better resistance to planted instructions, and leaves one security question open. The launch post says that on "coding benchmarks where models have no incentive to hold back, shortcut attempts dropped to just 2%", and that attack success on the Gray Swan Indirect Prompt Injection benchmark fell to 0.7% "across 15 adaptive attempts".

The model card is blunter about one risk: "We cannot rule out that Gemini 4 Argon reaches the cybersecurity CCL". CCL is a risk threshold the model card tests against. The card then says "We are therefore applying safeguards that we assess lower risk to below the cybersecurity CCL level".

## Should you switch your coding agents to Gemini 4 Argon?

Test it on long jobs first, and do not move everything at once. Our reasons: it tops most of Google's table, the coding rows are split, and the price doubles on 1 December 2026.
