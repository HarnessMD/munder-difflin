Take-Two's [press releases](https://ir.take2games.com/news-releases) stop at its 9 Nov results and Rockstar's [Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire) at 10 Nov, so no sales announcement came from 17 to 19 Nov. More money angles: [our GTA 6 hub](/blog/make-money-with-gta-6/).

## How many copies has GTA 6 sold so far?

Nobody official has said; the only counts are Sensor Tower estimates of console preorders, and they climb every few days. [Metro reported](https://metro.co.uk/2026/11/04/rockstar-will-take-videos-gta-6-story-spoilers-even-launch-29765723/) on 4 Nov 2026 that Sensor Tower counted "over $750 million (£555 million) in pre-order sales, with 7.71 million copies sold, as of November 3, 2026". [TweakTown reported](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/114626/gta-6-pre-orders-break-dollars1-billion-with-10-million-sales-analyst-firm-estimates/index.html) on 11 Nov 2026 that the estimate passed $1 billion and up to 10.3 million copies, net of returns, 75% on PlayStation. [Local 12 reported](https://local12.com/news/entertainment/grand-theft-auto-vi-game-tops-1b-in-pre-sales-with-11-million-copies-sold-rockstar-games-gta) more than 11 million on 13 Nov 2026. Sensor Tower's own 17 Nov report says preorder revenue "reached $1.1B across PlayStation and Xbox worldwide as of November 10" on 11.3 million copies. Its [live tracker](https://sensortower.com/grand-theft-auto-VI/sales-tracker) is "updated weekly", per its [August post](https://sensortower.com/blog/the-art-of-the-steal-gtavi-is-coming-video-game-insights-sales-tracker).

## Has GTA 6 broken a sales record?

It has broken two store preorder records, in the stores' own words, and neither came with a number. Sony's [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-ps5-starting-today/) said on 10 Nov 2026 that "Grand Theft Auto VI is now the most pre-ordered game in the history of PlayStation Store". Microsoft's [Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/10/grand-theft-auto-6-comes-to-xbox-cloud-gaming-november-19/) called it "the most pre-ordered title of all time on the Microsoft Store" the same day.

The third record is attention: [tbreak reports](https://tbreak.com/gta-6-streaming-record-before-launch/) (18 Nov 2026), citing preliminary Streams Charts data, more than 7.18 million concurrent livestream viewers within three hours of the game becoming available, a record that "counts people watching livestreams, not copies sold". Sensor Tower's August post says GTA V "saw pre-orders of 7M" in 2013, and its 17 Nov report calls $1.1 billion "the largest pre-launch total on record". Our view: GTA 6's estimates look bigger, but an estimate is not a record until Take-Two reports one.

## What has Take-Two actually said?

Take-Two has given guidance and adjectives, not a GTA 6 number. Its [release of 9 Nov 2026](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reports-results-fiscal-4) "reiterates expectation for Net Bookings of $8.0 to $8.2 billion" for the year to March 2027. In the [prepared remarks](https://ir.take2games.com/static-files/a45ff316-72c4-4255-b123-aace2e41ad1a) that day, Strauss Zelnick said preorders "are tracking far in excess of our initial expectations, and we know that the numbers will speak for themselves soon enough". CFO Lainie Goldstein put net bookings for October to December 2026, the quarter with launch day, "to range from $3.35 to $3.5 billion, compared to $1.76 billion in the prior year". That is CFO for "about double".

Net bookings count what is "sold digitally or sold-in physically during the period", so a boxed copy counts when a shop buys it, not when a player does, and the release gives no GTA 6 line.

After two delays ("Coming 2025", then 26 May 2026), launch [moved to 19 Nov 2026](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak3ak31a49a221/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-now-set-to-launch-november-19-2026), inside the quarter ending 31 December 2026. Our view: the first official number comes in an announcement or with that quarter's results; no results date was posted on 19 Nov. The [marketing](/blog/gta-6-marketing/) is a lesson of its own.

## Has GTA 6 made its money back?

No source can say, because none compares the game's revenue with its cost. Rockstar and Take-Two have not published a budget for GTA 6, so any budget figure you see online is unconfirmed. The closest Take-Two comes is direction, not size: on a management basis, operating expenses for October to December 2026 are "expected to grow by approximately 21% year-over-year, which is primarily driven by marketing costs to support the launch of Grand Theft Auto VI".

Zelnick will not call it either: "we never claim success before it's earned", he told Variety, as [Insider Gaming reports](https://insider-gaming.com/gta-6-presales-far-in-excess-take-two-expectations/) (9 Nov 2026).

## How do you read a launch number?

Ask five questions before repeating any GTA 6 figure.

1. **Who counted?** Take-Two, a store, a tracker, or an outlet repeating one. A forecast is not a count.
2. **Counted what?** Copies or dollars, gross or net of returns. Dollars move with the [edition mix](/blog/gta-6-editions-price/) and [refunds](/blog/gta-6-refund/).
3. **As of when?** [LEVEL UP](https://www.levelup.com/en/news/gta-vi-shatters-playstation-and-xbox-pre-order-records-but-warnings-over-download-times-have-been-issued/) printed 9.69 million on 12 Nov 2026, a day after TweakTown's 10.3 million. Same tracker, older snapshot.
4. **One game or the company?** A [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/the-level-of-pre-orders-is-unprecedented-take-two-reportedly-made-usd1-39-billion-in-sales-during-gta-6s-pre-order-period) headline on 7 Aug 2026 tied a $1.39 billion Take-Two quarter to GTA 6 preorders; its own text says the figure "wasn't broken down by title".
5. **Sales or attention?** tbreak's 7.18 million viewers (18 Nov 2026) are an audience, not 7.18 million buyers.
