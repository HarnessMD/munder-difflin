## What are the GTA 6 review scores right now?

It is 99 on both big aggregators, as of 19 Nov 2026. [Metacritic](https://www.metacritic.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi/) shows a PlayStation 5 Metascore of 99, "Based on 150 Critic Reviews", with "100% Positive". Xbox Series X is also 99, from 14 reviews. The user score reads "tbd": the game launches on 19 Nov ([Rockstar's Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/517572321a549a/watch-the-grand-theft-auto-vi-launch-trailer)).

[OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/15398/grand-theft-auto-vi) shows a 99 Top Critic Average, "99% Critics Recommend" and 88 critic reviews.

Metacritic's [help center](https://metacritichelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14482725990679-Last-week-MOVIE-TITLE-had-a-METASCORE-of-67-but-now-it-says-75-What-s-up-with-that-Am-I-hallucinating) says a Metascore can change "sometimes many times within a day", in an article whose title ends "Am I hallucinating?"

## How do Metacritic and OpenCritic calculate the score?

Metacritic takes a weighted average; OpenCritic takes a plain one. Metacritic's [about page](https://www.metacritic.com/about-us/) says "The weighted average of those scores results in a single number and color, the Metascore." Its [help center](https://metacritichelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15456539457175-Why-does-the-term-weighted-average-matter) adds: "we assign more importance, or weight, to some critics and publications than others, based on their quality and overall stature." The weights themselves? Its help article "Can you tell me how each of the different critics are weighted in your formula?" answers, in full: "[Absolutely not.](https://metacritichelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14482824029079-Can-you-tell-me-how-each-of-the-different-critics-are-weighted-in-your-formula)"

OpenCritic's [FAQ](https://opencritic.com/faq) says "The Top Critic Average is calculated by taking the simple average score of all numeric reviews written by top critics after normalizing the score on a 0-100 scale." Unscored reviews are "not included in any score calculations", so Polygon's unscored review does not touch the 99.

Do this, not that: compare on the same site and platform, and read the review count.

## What do critics praise, and what do they complain about?

The praise is the world; the complaints are frame rate, slow stretches and a familiar core.

- **Praised: detail, freedom, presentation.** IGN: "Exquisitely detailed". TheSixthAxis: "so much more freedom with how to tackle missions". Push Square: "The audiovisual presentation is a new high for the industry".
- **Flagged: 30 fps.** Rockstar is "targeting 30 frames per second" ([Newswire, 10 Nov 2026](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/89834o94713o21/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-now)), and VGC writes that "Minor performance issues don’t dull the sparkle".
- **Flagged: pace and familiarity.** ScreenHub, the 80, found "large sections that are boring and too limited". SECTOR.sk gave 100 and still wrote that "the core gameplay remains largely familiar".

On Metacritic that adds up to 150 positive, 0 mixed and 0 negative.

## How does GTA 6 compare with GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2?

On Metacritic, GTA 6 scores two points higher than both. Original platforms, read 19 Nov 2026:

- **GTA VI** (PS5): 99 from 150 reviews.
- **[GTA V](https://www.metacritic.com/game/grand-theft-auto-v/)** (17 Sep 2013): 97 on Xbox 360 from 58 reviews.
- **[Red Dead Redemption 2](https://www.metacritic.com/game/red-dead-redemption-2/)** (26 Oct 2018): 97 on PS4 from 99 reviews.

On OpenCritic the gap is wider: [Red Dead Redemption 2](https://opencritic.com/game/3717/red-dead-redemption-2) has a 95 Top Critic Average and [GTA V](https://opencritic.com/game/163/grand-theft-auto-v) a 92. OpenCritic dates GTA V's entry 18 Nov 2014, and its FAQ says records before 1 October 2015 "are meant to establish a baseline and not be a 100% accurate historical record."

The 99 also matches [The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time](https://www.metacritic.com/game/the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/): 99 on Nintendo 64, from 22 reviews, in 1998. GTA 6 got there with almost seven times as many critics.

## Is GTA 6 worth it?

Our view: yes, if you like open worlds and can live with 30 fps, because the evidence is unusually one sided: 150 positive Metacritic reviews and 99% of OpenCritic's critics recommending it. PlayStation's [US store](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/) lists Standard at $79.99 on 19 Nov 2026; the [editions and price guide](/blog/gta-6-editions-price/) has the rest.

We think three groups should check first:

- **Frame rate fans.** Rockstar's target is 30 fps; the PS5 Pro adds "improved clarity" and ray traced shadows.
- **Online players.** Rockstar calls GTA VI "a single player experience" ([Newswire, 24 Jun 2026](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/5171972o3ak5oa/pre-order-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-june-25)).
- **Busy people.** One Rockstar developer's playthrough took 80 hours, [TechRadar reports](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/gta-6-will-be-a-massive-game-and-rockstars-longest-yet-as-the-studio-confirms-it-can-take-up-to-80-hours-to-beat) (28 Aug 2026); see [how long GTA 6 takes to beat](/blog/gta-6-how-long-to-beat/).

Player reviews are not in yet; if you trust players over critics, wait for the user score.
