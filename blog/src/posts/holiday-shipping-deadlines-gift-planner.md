Inside the United States, ship Christmas 2026 gifts by Thursday, 17 Dec for USPS Ground Advantage. The latest is Wednesday, 23 Dec for UPS Next Day Air or a FedEx overnight service, Yahoo Shopping reports (24 Nov 2026). Give each gift a budget and an order by date, and the list can land on time. ## What are the last days to ship for Christmas 2026?

Thursday, 17 Dec is the first USPS date to circle, and Wednesday, 23 Dec is the last overnight date at UPS and FedEx. USPS announced its dates on [22 Sep 2026](https://about.usps.com/newsroom/national-releases/2026/0922-usps-recommends-2026-holiday-mailing-shipping-dates.htm), "for expected delivery before Dec. 25"; its [holiday shipping page](https://www.usps.com/holiday/holiday-shipping-dates.htm), checked 27 Nov 2026, adds the weekdays.

The FedEx and UPS dates come from one news report: [Yahoo Shopping reports](https://shopping.yahoo.com/gift-ideas/articles/christmas-gifts-running-2026-shipping-063029147.html), on 24 Nov 2026, that both are for packages "to arrive by Christmas Eve," 24 Dec. Confirm on [fedex.com](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/holiday.html) or [ups.com](https://www.ups.com/) before you ship.

| Carrier | Service | Ship by |
|---|---|---|
| USPS | USPS Ground Advantage | Thursday, 17 Dec |
| USPS | First-Class Mail | Thursday, 17 Dec |
| USPS | Priority Mail | Friday, 18 Dec |
| USPS | Priority Mail Express | Saturday, 19 Dec |
| FedEx | FedEx Ground Economy | Tuesday, 15 Dec |
| FedEx | FedEx Ground and Home Delivery | Thursday, 17 Dec (five day transit) to Wednesday, 23 Dec (one day transit) |
| FedEx | FedEx Express Saver | Monday, 21 Dec |
| FedEx | FedEx 2Day | Tuesday, 22 Dec |
| FedEx | First Overnight, Priority Overnight, Standard Overnight | Wednesday, 23 Dec |
| UPS | UPS Ground | No fixed date; check its Calculate Time and Cost tool |
| UPS | UPS 3 Day Select | Monday, 21 Dec |
| UPS | UPS 2nd Day Air | Tuesday, 22 Dec |
| UPS | UPS Next Day Air | Wednesday, 23 Dec |

USPS dates are for the lower 48 states and are "recommended": the "actual delivery date may vary and is based on origin, destination, Post Office™ acceptance time, and other conditions." Yahoo Shopping reports, on 24 Nov 2026, that the published dates "don't guarantee every shipment will arrive on time."

For Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico and the U.S. territories, the other USPS dates hold, but USPS gives no single date for USPS Ground Advantage there, only a service expectation of 10 days or more. USPS also notes Hanukkah runs 4 Dec to 12 Dec this year, so ship those gifts earlier.

## How early should you ship gifts abroad?

About two weeks before Christmas. The USPS page, checked 27 Nov 2026, gives Wednesday, 9 Dec for First-Class Package International Service and Priority Mail International, and Wednesday, 16 Dec for Priority Mail Express International, for every region it lists, from Canada and Mexico to Europe and Africa.

## How long can you return a gift?

Each store sets its own window; the Amazon and Target holiday return dates are in our [Black Friday deal checker](/blog/black-friday-deal-checker/), so copy one into a Return by note beside each gift.

## What goes on your gift list?

One row per person, dates first. Copy this:

| Person | Budget | Idea | Order by | Ship by (service) | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gran | Your number | Jigsaw puzzle | 12 Dec | 17 Dec (USPS Ground Advantage) | Idea |
| Sam, abroad | Your number | Paperback | 4 Dec | 9 Dec (Priority Mail International) | Ordered |

Our view: set Order by a few days before Ship by, so the gift reaches you with time to wrap. If the store ships straight to the person, use its own last order date.

## How do you work back from 25 Dec?

Tick each step as its day comes. Five steps, each from the dates above:

1. **Wednesday, 9 Dec:** anything going abroad, by First-Class Package International Service or Priority Mail International.
2. **Thursday, 17 Dec:** USPS Ground Advantage and First-Class Mail, for cards and gifts that can travel slowly.
3. **Friday, 18 Dec and Saturday, 19 Dec:** USPS Priority Mail, then Priority Mail Express.
4. **Monday, 21 Dec and Tuesday, 22 Dec:** UPS 3 Day Select or FedEx Express Saver, then UPS 2nd Day Air or FedEx 2Day, for arrival by 24 Dec, as Yahoo Shopping reports (24 Nov 2026).
5. **Wednesday, 23 Dec:** the last call, UPS Next Day Air or a FedEx overnight service, per the same report. After that, wrap it and hand it over in person.

## What does the agent team do?

Five agents read public pages and write notes and drafts; you choose every gift, you sign in, you pay, you press buy, you write the addresses and you ship. No agent signs in to a store, pays, buys, ships, stores card details or holds anyone's address.

* **List Keeper** keeps the gift list table, first names only.
* **Gift Scout** finds two ideas per person inside the budget.
* **Budget Keeper** totals the list and flags any row over budget.
* **Deadline Keeper** suggests a service per gift, writes its order by date and reminds you.
* **Card Writer** drafts a card note per person.

```
List Keeper: Turn my notes into the gift list table. First names only.
Gift Scout: Suggest two ideas per person under their budget, with links to public pages.
Budget Keeper: Total the list and flag any row over its budget.
Deadline Keeper: Suggest a service per gift from the carrier dates, write an order by date, remind me two days before each ship by date.
Card Writer: Draft a three line card note per person. I will edit them.
```

You get back the filled gift list, an order by date per gift and card drafts. We think the Card Writer earns its seat by your third aunt.
