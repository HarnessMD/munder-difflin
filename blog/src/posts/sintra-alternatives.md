Our view: Marblism is the closest Sintra alternative, because it also sells a cast of named AI employees to small business owners on one plan. Sintra gives you 12 helpers on the web and in mobile apps, paid up front for one, three or 12 months. Checked 14 Nov 2026.

We make [Munder Difflin](https://harnessmd.com/download), which is free and open source: a desktop app that runs a team of AI agents for all kinds of knowledge work, coding included, on your own computer. Our own agents are in [AI agent use cases from our office](/blog/ai-agent-use-cases-our-office/); more lists are in the [Comparisons hub](/blog/topics/comparisons/).

## What is Sintra?

Sintra gives a small business a team of named AI employees, each with one job. Its [help center](https://help.sintra.ai/en/articles/12266334-what-is-sintra-ai), in an article dated 4 Sep 2026, says "At the center of everything is Brain AI, a shared knowledge hub that learns your brand voice, stores your documents, and keeps every AI employee aligned with your business."

## What do Sintra's 12 helpers do?

Each helper owns one business job. The [home page](https://sintra.ai), read 14 Nov 2026, lists Buddy for business development, Cassie for support, Commet for eCommerce, Dexter for data, Emmie for email, Gigi as a growth coach, Penn for copy, Scouty for hiring, Seomi for SEO, Soshie for social media, Vizzy as an assistant and Milli for sales. You "Type your instructions into chat, and it'll delegate to the best AI employee for the job", per the [pricing page](https://sintra.ai/pricing).

## How does Sintra run?

It runs in your browser and in mobile apps. The home page says "Available 24/7 on web and mobile", and its [help center](https://help.sintra.ai/en/) links both app stores. Recurring tasks "can run as often as every 10 minutes."

## How much does Sintra cost?

Sintra sells one plan with all 12 helpers and 250 credits a month, paid up front. On 14 Nov 2026 the [pricing page](https://sintra.ai/pricing) showed $15.60 a month on the 12 month plan, $23.60 a month on the 3 month plan and $48.50 for one month. It has a 14 day money back guarantee "(but no free trial)". Big jobs use more: "Websites cost 30 credits for a simple build, and 300+ for more complex online stores."

## Can you bring your own model to Sintra?

Not for the 12 helpers. No Sintra page we opened on 14 Nov 2026 offers them a model choice or your own API key. The pricing page says its helpers beat "a standard chat model like ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini".

## Why look for a Sintra alternative?

Sintra's own pricing page lists when it "won't work", including "An Enterprise solution" and "A power tool with AI coding ability". The prepaid terms and no free trial matter too. Otherwise, this list is for people comparing tools like Sintra before they pick.

## 1. Marblism: the closest match to Sintra

Marblism sells seven named AI employees on one plan. Its [pricing page](https://www.marblism.com/pricing), read 14 Nov 2026, says "Plans start at $24/month on the yearly option", and every plan includes all 7 employees and 50 hours of work. On 4 Nov 2026 it [launched Rooms](https://www.marblism.com/news), where several employees and your teammates "work on the same project together." Its own index ranks Marblism first and Sintra third, as self reviews do. We think it suits owners who want a phone receptionist; see [Marblism alternatives](/blog/marblism-alternatives/).

## 2. Munder Difflin: a team of agents on your computer or in the cloud

Munder Difflin runs a team of AI agents for research, writing, ops and code. [harnessmd.com](https://harnessmd.com), read 14 Nov 2026, says "Use subscriptions you already pay for to run agents in your personal simulation." Its [README](https://github.com/HarnessMD/munder-difflin) that day says "each agent gets long-term memory, a mailbox, and a desk on a 2D office floor". Work comes in from Slack or a webhook, and agents gain tools through MCP servers, skills and plugins. The Cloud Office runs your agents in the cloud, always on, so work goes on with your computer off; a mobile app is coming soon. Setup is in [how to install and use Munder Difflin](/blog/how-to-install-and-use-munder-difflin/).

## 3. Lindy: one assistant with a model per task

Lindy is one AI employee across your tools, not a named cast. Its [home page](https://www.lindy.ai), read 14 Nov 2026, says "I work in Slack, email, text and your calendar, with 3,000+ integrations + MCP." On 14 Nov 2026 the [pricing page](https://www.lindy.ai/pricing) gave $50 in free credits for 7 days, then Team from $29.99 per user a month, or $49.99 in another version of the page, which lists "Choose your model for each task". We think it suits small teams that live in Slack.

## 4. Claude Cowork: if you already pay for Claude

Cowork hands longer jobs to one assistant, Claude. Anthropic's [help page](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork), read 14 Nov 2026, says it "brings Claude Code's agentic capabilities to knowledge work beyond coding" and "runs your sessions remotely in the cloud", on paid plans only. [Claude's pricing page](https://claude.com/pricing) listed Pro at $20 if billed monthly on 14 Nov 2026. We think it suits people already on Claude; see [Claude Cowork alternatives](/blog/claude-cowork-alternatives/).

## 5. Relevance AI: when you need the enterprise version

Relevance AI builds agent teams for larger companies, the case Sintra rules out. Its [pricing page](https://relevanceai.com/pricing), read 14 Nov 2026, shows only an Enterprise plan, with "Talk to sales". On 30 Oct 2026 its [changelog](https://relevanceai.com/docs/changelog) let Enterprise admins "choose which models their organization can use". We think it suits companies with an IT team.

## How to choose

Match the tool to how your business works. Our view:

- Marblism for a named cast with a phone receptionist.
- Munder Difflin for a team of AI agents for research, writing, ops and code, on your computer or in the Cloud Office.
- Lindy for one assistant across Slack, email and calls, with a model per task.
- Claude Cowork if you already pay for Claude.
- Relevance AI for an enterprise rollout with model controls.
- Stay on Sintra for 12 helpers on one prepaid plan.
