Six GTA 6 YouTube channel ideas work at launch, and every one runs on your own play: launch news, map guides, spoiler free mission help, comparisons, short tips and real places. Checked 16 Nov 2026. You play, record and voice. An AI agent team can do the research, scripts, rough cuts and tracking around you.

Rockstar's own page says Grand Theft Auto VI is "Coming November 19, 2026" on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S ([Rockstar](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI), read 16 Nov 2026). The team in this guide runs in [Munder Difflin](https://harnessmd.com/download), free and open source: a desktop app that runs a team of AI agents for all kinds of knowledge work, coding included, on your own computer. ## Which GTA 6 channels can you start at launch?

You can start six, and each one runs on footage you record and facts Rockstar publishes.

### 1. Launch news

Format: short videos on each Rockstar Newswire post, in your voice. The newest Newswire item on Rockstar's GTA VI page is "Pre-Load Grand Theft Auto VI Now", dated November 10, 2026. A news watcher agent checks on a schedule and drafts a script with every source linked. Yours: checking each line against Rockstar's post, recording and publishing.

### 2. Map and secrets guides

Format: one place or hidden detail per video, found in your own sessions. Agents turn your notes into an outline, log timestamps and draft chapter markers. Yours: finding it, filming it and confirming it.

### 3. Mission help without endings

Format: how to beat a hard mission, with story scenes cut out. Reported Rockstar rules remove endings, so keep the help about the play. Agents draft steps from your notes and flag story words you listed as spoilers. Yours: playing the mission and approving the cut.

### 4. Vehicle or weapon comparisons

Format: two or three vehicles or weapons tested side by side in your own game. Agents build the table from numbers you log and list what is still untested. Yours: every test and the verdict. Never fill a gap with another channel's stats.

### 5. Shorts or TikTok tips

Format: one tip per short vertical video, cut from your recordings. A coding agent such as Claude Code can run ffmpeg to trim and reframe a clip you marked, and a transcription tool can draft captions. Yours: picking the moment, fixing captions and posting. Paid clipping campaigns are a separate job: see the [clipping farm guide](/blog/gta-6-clipping-farm/).

### 6. Real places versus the game

Format: your own photos, or licensed ones, beside your game captures. Rockstar's page places the story in "Vice City, USA" and "the state of Leonida" and names no real city or state, so present every match as your view. Agents research the real spot and link each source. Yours: photos, captures and opinion.

## How do these channels earn?

They earn only after a platform accepts you, and payouts depend on views, campaign budgets and approval, so they can be zero. YouTube's [Partner Program page](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851), read 16 Nov 2026, lists two routes: "Get 1,000 subscribers with 4,000 qualified watch hours in the last 12 months" or "Get 1,000 subscribers with 10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days". Even then, "your channel will go through a standard review process".

## What does Rockstar allow?

Reports say Rockstar lets fans post gameplay but removes story spoilers. [tbreak reports](https://tbreak.com/gta-6-spoilers-rockstar-video-takedown-policy/) (4 Nov 2026, updated 8 Nov) that Rockstar's video policy, last updated January 2025, covers GTA 6. Removed: endings, edited cutscene and dialogue compilations, isolated cinematics and footage from before release. Allowed: cutscenes inside a longer playthrough and narrated Let's Play videos. [Metro reports](https://metro.co.uk/2026/11/04/rockstar-will-take-videos-gta-6-story-spoilers-even-launch-29765723/) (4 Nov 2026) the same line: "isolated videos of the game's cinematics will be removed".

tbreak also reports that the policy page "frames that permission around non-commercial use", and, citing Prepublish's reading, that Rockstar can take material down "at any time". Read [Rockstar's policy page](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/200153756/Policy-on-posting-copyrighted-Rockstar-Games-material) yourself before you earn from footage. This is not legal advice.

Our view: build each channel so it survives one video coming down. House rules for every workflow here: no fake engagement, no bought views, no reuploads of other people's videos without permission, no story spoilers. You approve each piece and post it under your own account.

## What does the agent team look like?

It is five agents with one job each, coordinated on a task board in Munder Difflin while you play.

- **News watcher**: checks the Newswire on a schedule and opens a task when something is new.
- **Script writer**: turns your notes and links into a draft you rewrite in your voice.
- **Editor**: runs ffmpeg on clips you marked.
- **Titles helper**: drafts titles and a thumbnail brief from your captures.
- **Analyst**: reads the channel numbers you export and says which format earned its time.

The [Munder Difflin README](https://github.com/HarnessMD/munder-difflin), read 16 Nov 2026, lists "Kanban tasks with dependencies, scheduled missions + heartbeat", so the script waits for the watcher. It also lists "Slack & webhooks: message a channel or POST a webhook", so a draft reaches you where you read messages. Each agent runs an engine such as Claude Code, Codex or Gemini CLI. The Cloud Office runs agents in the cloud, always on; [harnessmd.com](https://harnessmd.com), read 16 Nov 2026, says "Close your laptop and a Cloud Office keeps going". The task board and automations are part of "the Pro workspace", and "The free plan lets you use Pro features up to a monthly allowance of work tokens".
