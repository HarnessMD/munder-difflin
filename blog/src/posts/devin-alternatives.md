Our view: OpenHands is the best open source Devin alternative, and OpenAI Codex is the closest hosted one. People look elsewhere to self host, to read the code, or to use a plan they already pay for: Devin Pro, at $20 a month, adds "Access to cloud agents (Devin Cloud)", checked 21 Nov 2026.

We make [Munder Difflin](https://harnessmd.com/download), which is free and open source: a desktop app that runs a team of AI agents for all kinds of knowledge work, coding included, on your own computer. More lists: the [best AI coding agents](/blog/best-ai-coding-agents/) and [Herdr alternatives](/blog/herdr-alternatives/).

## What is Devin?

Devin is Cognition's coding agent: it takes a task and works on its own until a pull request is ready. The [Devin site](https://devin.ai), read 21 Nov 2026, says "Meet Devin, your team's autonomous software engineer" and "Devin runs in the cloud or on your machine, tests in its own browser, and won't stop until the PR is ready to merge". It "Keeps working when your laptop is closed", and you can "Tag @Devin in Slack, Teams, and more."

## How much does Devin cost?

Devin has a free plan, three priced plans and Enterprise. Pro adds "Access to cloud agents (Devin Cloud)" and Max adds "Access to Devin Bots".

## What are the best Devin alternatives?

Six tools that take a task and work on their own, as of 21 Nov 2026: OpenHands, Munder Difflin, OpenAI Codex, GitHub Copilot, Google Jules and OpenComputer. People are looking: a [Show HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50581403) on 20 Nov 2026 presented OpenComputer as "an open source alternative to Devin".

## 1. OpenHands: the strongest open source pick

We think OpenHands is the most complete open source pick. Its [README](https://github.com/OpenHands/OpenHands), read 21 Nov 2026, says Agent Canvas "turns your coding agents into a self-hosted, always-on engineering team". It "runs locally on your machine by default", with Docker, VMs and OpenHands Cloud as options. It is MIT licensed with 93,931 stars on 21 Nov 2026, and [v1.43.0](https://github.com/OpenHands/OpenHands/releases/tag/v1.43.0) came out on 20 Nov 2026. Self hosting is "Free" on the [pricing page](https://www.openhands.dev/pricing); Teams cost $500/month with "10 users included" on 21 Nov 2026. The README says "Use with any LLM"; for an open weights model, see [Inkling 2 Mini](/blog/inkling-2-mini/). More in [OpenHands alternatives](/blog/openhands-alternatives/).

## 2. Munder Difflin: a team of agents for all your work

Munder Difflin runs a team of agents, not one agent for code. Its [README](https://github.com/HarnessMD/munder-difflin), read 21 Nov 2026, says "Use the subscriptions you already pay for to run an office of AI agents on your own computer." You hand them work "in plain words: research, writing, analysis, planning, support, operations, and code too." Each agent can run an engine such as Claude Code, Codex or Gemini CLI, use MCP servers, skills and plugins, and take work from Slack or webhooks. The Cloud Office runs your agents in the cloud, always on, so work goes on with your computer off. Setup: [how to install and use Munder Difflin](/blog/how-to-install-and-use-munder-difflin/).

## 3. OpenAI Codex: cloud tasks on a ChatGPT plan

Codex runs coding tasks in the cloud on ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise plans. The [Codex Cloud docs](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/cloud), read 21 Nov 2026, say "Give Codex coding tasks to work through in the cloud, from investigating bugs to building features." Each task "has its own workspace and can keep working while your computer is asleep". On 21 Nov 2026 the [pricing page](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/pricing) listed cloud chats on Plus at $20/month and Pro from $100/month. The [Codex CLI](https://github.com/openai/codex) is Apache 2.0; the cloud side is an OpenAI service.

## 4. GitHub Copilot: Copilot in the cloud

GitHub Copilot takes work from a chat or an issue and runs it in a cloud sandbox or on GitHub Actions. [GitHub's docs](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/concepts/copilot-surfaces/copilot-on-github), read 21 Nov 2026, say Copilot "can answer questions about your repositories, plan and make changes, review pull requests, and automate repository tasks." Starting October 28, 2026, the new Copilot in the cloud experience "is available to users with a paid Copilot plan, subject to applicable organization and enterprise policies". On 21 Nov 2026 the [plans page](https://github.com/features/copilot/plans) listed Pro at $10 per user per month with "Access to Copilot in the cloud and code review".

## 5. Google Jules: a coding agent in a cloud VM

Jules is Google's coding agent, and it works in a cloud VM. The [Jules docs](https://jules.google/docs), read 21 Nov 2026, call it "an experimental coding agent that helps you fix bugs, add documentation, and build new features". The [Jules site](https://jules.google) says "Jules fetches your repository, clones it to a Cloud VM, and develops a plan". The base plan, "Powered by Gemini 2.5 Pro", allows "15 tasks per day" and "3 concurrent tasks". Paid plans "are accessed via a Google AI Plans subscription, which is currently available only for individual Google Accounts", per the [usage limits page](https://jules.google/docs/usage-limits); no Jules page showed a price on 21 Nov 2026.

## 6. OpenComputer: new, and built as a Devin alternative

OpenComputer is new as a Devin alternative. Its [README](https://github.com/diggerhq/opencomputer), [rewritten on 13 Nov 2026](https://github.com/diggerhq/opencomputer/commit/990a64e2064a8c1cd7f48d24fd15bfc5191113a3) and read 21 Nov 2026, says "The open-source Devin alternative: a coding agent your team works with in Slack, on its own computers, in your cloud or ours." You mention `@Computer` in a Slack thread, it "starts a session with Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode, depending on your configuration", and "the result lands as a pull request". It is Apache 2.0, with 766 stars on 21 Nov 2026; the latest release is [v0.6.0.50](https://github.com/diggerhq/opencomputer/releases/tag/v0.6.0.50) of 9 Nov 2026. Its cloud has a "3-day free trial with 600 work minutes".

## How to choose

Our view:

- Open source, on your own machine or servers: OpenHands.
- A team of agents for code, research, writing and ops, on your computer or in the Cloud Office: Munder Difflin.
- A ChatGPT plan: OpenAI Codex.
- Code on GitHub: GitHub Copilot.
- Google and Gemini: Jules.
- Devin's Slack flow, in open source: OpenComputer.
- Devin Bots and testing in its own browser: stay on Devin.
