## What is Eigent?

Eigent is an open source desktop app that runs AI agents on your computer, built on CAMEL-AI's open source project. Its [README](https://github.com/eigent-ai/eigent), read 14 Nov 2026, calls it "the open source Cowork desktop application".

## What is Eigent's workforce?

The workforce is a group of agents that split one task and work on its parts at once. The README says you can "Scale to multiple specialized agents that divide work, collaborate in parallel, and execute complex multi-step workflows together." The [docs](https://www.eigent.ai/docs) add: "If a task gets stuck or encounters uncertainty, Eigent will automatically request human input." Automations start agents on a schedule, a webhook or an app event.

## How does Eigent run, and which models does it use?

It runs as a desktop app for macOS, Windows and Linux, per its [download page](https://www.eigent.ai/download) on 14 Nov 2026. The README recommends local deployment with "no cloud account required", and local models through "vLLM, Ollama, LM Studio, etc." On the [pricing page](https://www.eigent.ai/pricing), the Free plan covers "BYOK and local models" and lists 16 supported models. Plus adds Claude Sonnet 5, Claude Fable 5.1, Claude Opus 5.5 and Grok 4.7.

## How much does Eigent cost, and is it open source?

The app is free and Apache 2.0, and the paid plans sell monthly task credits. On 14 Nov 2026 the pricing page listed Plus at $19.99 a month with 2,000 task credits, and Pro at $99.99 a month with 10,000. Teams read "Coming Soon". The [GitHub repo](https://github.com/eigent-ai/eigent) had 15,818 stars on 14 Nov 2026, and [v1.0.7](https://github.com/eigent-ai/eigent/releases/tag/v1.0.7) came out on 30 Oct 2026.

## 1. AionUi: the closest match to Eigent

AionUi is a free desktop app where a leader agent splits your request across teammate agents. Its [README](https://github.com/iOfficeAI/AionUi), read 14 Nov 2026, says "a Leader agent receives your instructions, breaks them into subtasks, and delegates to Teammate agents", which "execute in parallel". It also brings in agents you already run, such as Claude Code. It is Apache 2.0 for macOS, Windows and Linux, and its [last commit](https://github.com/iOfficeAI/AionUi/commits/main) landed on 5 Nov 2026. We think it suits Eigent users who want their own agents on the team. See [AionUi alternatives](/blog/aionui-alternatives/).

## 2. Munder Difflin: a team of agents on your computer or in the cloud

Munder Difflin runs a team of AI agents on your computer or in the Cloud Office. Its [README](https://github.com/HarnessMD/munder-difflin), read 14 Nov 2026, says it "coordinates a whole office of agents on your own machine", with agents "coordinated by your clone (Michael)". Agents run on engines such as Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI, and the README adds "bring-your-own keys and local LLMs". Work comes in from Slack or a webhook, and agents use MCP servers, skills and plugins. The Cloud Office runs your agents in the cloud, always on, so work goes on with your computer off. A mobile app is coming soon. We think it suits people who want one office of agents for all kinds of knowledge work. Setup is in [how to install and use Munder Difflin](/blog/how-to-install-and-use-munder-difflin/).

## 3. Rowboat: agents and people in one group chat

Rowboat puts your agents and your coworkers in shared group chats. Its [README](https://github.com/rowboatlabs/rowboat), read 14 Nov 2026, says "Spin up agents, add them to a group chat, and let them collaborate on shared goals. Free and open source." It runs on the web, iPhone and desktop, and you can self host Harbor, its server. [v1.0.9](https://github.com/rowboatlabs/rowboat/releases/tag/v1.0.9) of the Apache 2.0 repo came out on 11 Nov 2026. We think it suits teams where people and agents share the work. See [Rowboat alternatives](/blog/rowboat-alternatives/).

## 4. Claude Cowork: the paid option from Anthropic

Claude Cowork is Anthropic's agent for your files and tools, on a paid Claude plan. Its [product page](https://claude.com/product/cowork), read 14 Nov 2026, says "Big projects are split into chunks that run together" and "Close your laptop, it keeps going." Anthropic's [article](https://claude.com/resources/articles/cowork-is-now-claude) says Cowork and chat merged into one Claude, with a 12 Nov 2026 update for Claude Team. [Claude's pricing page](https://claude.com/pricing) listed Pro at $20 if billed monthly on 14 Nov 2026. We think it suits people who already pay for Claude. See [Claude Cowork alternatives](/blog/claude-cowork-alternatives/).

## 5. CrewAI: if you would rather write code

CrewAI is an open source Python framework for building teams of agents yourself. Its [README](https://github.com/crewAIInc/crewAI), read 14 Nov 2026, describes Crews as "Teams of AI agents with true autonomy and agency". It is MIT, and [1.15.41](https://github.com/crewAIInc/crewAI/releases/tag/1.15.41) came out on 12 Nov 2026. We think it suits developers who want the workforce idea in their own code. See our [CrewAI and AutoGen comparison](/blog/crewai-autogen-vs-a-local-agent-harness/).

## How to choose

Pick by how you want the work split. Our view:

- AionUi for a leader and teammates in one free desktop app.
- Munder Difflin for a team of AI agents for all kinds of knowledge work, on your computer or in the Cloud Office.
- Rowboat if people and agents share one chat.
- Claude Cowork if you already pay for Claude.
- CrewAI if you want to build the team in Python.
- Eigent if you want its workforce, credits and model list in one app.

More free picks sit in our list of [open source AI agents](/blog/open-source-ai-agents/).
