## What is AionUi?

AionUi is a free, open source desktop app where AI agents do work on your computer. Its [README](https://github.com/iOfficeAI/AionUi), read 9 Nov 2026, calls it "a Cowork platform where AI agents work alongside you on your computer". It has its own agent and also detects the CLIs you already have. The repo description names "Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode and 20+ more CLI Agent".

It runs on macOS, Windows and Linux. Its WebUI mode opens it "via browser from phone, tablet, or any computer", and chat integrations for Telegram, Lark, DingTalk and WeChat do the same job. The licence is Apache 2.0. The [latest release](https://github.com/iOfficeAI/AionUi/releases/latest), v2.2.2, came out on 9 Sep 2026, and [commits](https://github.com/iOfficeAI/AionUi/commits/main) kept landing through 5 Nov 2026. The repo had 34,156 stars on 9 Nov 2026. Stars measure interest, not use.

## How does AionUi Team mode work?

A Leader agent splits your request and hands the parts to Teammate agents. The README says the Leader "receives your instructions, breaks them into subtasks, and delegates to Teammate agents". Teammates "share results through an async mailbox, and write to a shared task board." One detail matters for parallel work: "all agents share the same folder".

## Why look for an AionUi alternative?

Mostly to compare tools like AionUi before you pick, and three facts can tip it. Slack and Discord are listed as "coming soon". Team mode puts every agent in one folder, and we think some teams will want files kept apart. And no release has shipped since 9 Sep 2026, though commits go on.

## 1. Orca: the closest match

Orca is a free desktop app that runs several agent CLIs at once. Its [README](https://github.com/stablyai/orca), read 9 Nov 2026, says "Run Claude Code, Codex, and any other coding agent in parallel, each in its own worktree." One agent can also lead: "It splits the work, starts worker agents in their own workspaces". The [Orca site](https://www.onorca.dev) answers "Is it free?" with "Free and open source." It suits people whose agents mostly write code. See [Orca vs Munder Difflin](/blog/orca-vs-munder-difflin/).

## 2. Munder Difflin: a team for all kinds of work

Munder Difflin is ours, and like AionUi it covers more than code. Agents run while your computer runs. Its [README](https://github.com/HarnessMD/munder-difflin), read 9 Nov 2026, describes "A GOD orchestrator you talk to. It routes tasks, adjudicates traffic, and escalates only what needs a human." Agents run on engines such as Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI, take work from Slack or a webhook, and add tools through MCP servers, skills and plugins. A Cloud Office and a mobile app are coming soon. It suits mixed work: research, writing, ops and code. Setup is in [how to install and use Munder Difflin](/blog/how-to-install-and-use-munder-difflin/).

## 3. OpenWork: the Cowork style desktop app

OpenWork is a desktop app where agents work on your own files, built on OpenCode. Its [README](https://github.com/different-ai/openwork), read 9 Nov 2026, calls it "the free, open-source alternative to Claude Cowork and Codex". The desktop app is MIT licensed; the team control plane has a separate EE licence. Its [pricing page](https://openworklabs.com/pricing), read 9 Nov 2026, lists Free at $0 for "up to 5 users" and Team at $10 per user a month. It suits teams that share skills and MCP servers.

## 4. Claude Cowork: Anthropic's own

Claude Cowork is Anthropic's agent for office work. Its [help page](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork), read 9 Nov 2026, says "Claude's work runs on Anthropic's servers, in an isolated environment". It needs a paid plan: Pro, Max, Ultra, Team or Enterprise. On 9 Nov 2026, Anthropic's [pricing page](https://claude.com/pricing) listed Pro at $20 a month billed monthly, or $17 a month billed yearly. AionUi's README calls Cowork "macOS Only"; the help page now lists Claude Desktop for macOS, Windows and Linux (beta). It suits people who want one vendor and no setup. More in [Claude Cowork alternatives](/blog/claude-cowork-alternatives/).

## 5. Paperclip: if you run agents like a company

Paperclip is a self hosted server that runs a team of agents as a business. Its [README](https://github.com/paperclipai/paperclip), read 9 Nov 2026, says it "orchestrates a team of AI agents to run a business". It adds: "Open source. Self-hosted. No Paperclip account required." The licence is MIT, v2026.1106.0 came out on 6 Nov 2026, and Paperclip Cloud has a waitlist. It suits people who track agents against goals and budgets. See [Paperclip alternatives](/blog/paperclip-alternatives/).

## How to choose

Our view: pick by the shape of your work.

- Orca for the closest swap: one desktop app, many agent CLIs, a worktree each.
- Munder Difflin for a team of agents on research, writing, ops and code, run from one orchestrator.
- OpenWork if your team wants a Cowork style app with shared skills and MCP servers.
- Claude Cowork if you already pay for Claude and want Anthropic to run it.
- Paperclip for goals, budgets and an org chart around your agents.
- AionUi itself if Team mode, the WebUI and Telegram or WeChat access fit how you work.
