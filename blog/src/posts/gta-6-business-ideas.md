## What rule applies to every GTA 6 business?

Rockstar's own terms apply to all nine: the game is licensed for personal fun, and its logos are not yours to sell. [Rockstar's Terms of Service](https://www.rockstargames.com/eula), last updated 28 Feb 2025 and read 19 Nov 2026, license the game "for your personal, non-commercial enjoyment". They define Content to include "trademarks, logos, trade names, trade dress", and you may not "commercialize, or otherwise exploit the Services (including the Content)" without "separate, express written terms provided by Rockstar". Section 6.1 also rules out any market to "rent, sell, share, exchange, or transfer any Virtual Items or Accounts".

So every idea below sells something yours. Footage has its own rules: read [Rockstar's policy page](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/200153756/Policy-on-posting-copyrighted-Rockstar-Games-material) before anything you sell touches gameplay. This is not legal advice.

## How does each idea work, and what do agents do?

Each idea is one job you own plus prep an agent can draft.

- **Deals newsletter.** Weekly console, storage and headset deals. Amazon requires "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases." The FTC says "The closer the disclosure is to your recommendation, the better." Agents check prices and links; you pick and send.
- **Setup and coaching.** Paid sessions that get a new owner set up. PlayStation's terms say users "should safeguard their Account sign-in ID, password, passkey", so the owner types every password. Agents draft checklists; you run the session.
- **Print shop.** Your own designs on shirts and posters, via Printify, TikTok Shop or your own store. Printify makes you warrant the content "is owned by You or that You have all necessary rights". Agents draft listings and size files; you design.
- **Sponsor matchmaking.** You pair small creators with fitting brands, for a fee in writing. YouTube says creators "have to let YouTube know by selecting the paid promotion button". Agents build shortlists; you sign. Creators also need [a content kit](/blog/gta-6-content-creator/).
- **Subtitles and translation.** You translate a creator's own commentary. YouTube says subtitles reach "viewers who speak another language". Agents draft translations; a fluent person checks every line. Skip story dialogue.
- **Tracker or data site.** Dated public numbers and news. Google calls "Scraping feeds, search results, or other content to generate many pages" scaled content abuse "where little value is provided to users". Agents gather sources; you write the takeaways ([tracker build](/blog/gta-6-tracker/)).
- **Overlay and template shop.** Overlays and thumbnail templates in your style. Gumroad's pricing page, read 19 Nov 2026, lists "10% + 50¢ direct, 30% via Discover"; its rules prohibit unauthorized copies of "video games". Agents export sizes; you draw.
- **Discord with paid tiers.** Server Subscriptions for your coaching calls. Discord bars monetizing "Intellectual property of other rights holders without written permission", and says "You must be 18 years or older." Agents draft welcome posts; you moderate ([Discord guide](/blog/gta-6-discord-server/)).
- **Discord bot.** Spoiler tags and Newswire alerts, with a paid tier. Discord's rule: "Your app must be verified." Agents write and test the code; you hold the token and ship.

## Which GTA 6 business ideas did we cut?

We cut paid LAN nights, paid entry tournaments and account sales, because the terms rule them out unless Rockstar agrees in writing. Rockstar's terms say you will not use the game "in connection with any wager of any money or other thing of value" without written terms. PlayStation's terms say a store purchase is "a personal license to use that product for private, non-commercial use". We think a ticket to play is commercial use.

Our view: host the launch party for friends, free, and sell something of yours on Monday.
