Our view: budget for the December price, because the launch price is a seven week offer. Gemini 4 pricing on 13 Oct 2026 is $2.00 input and $10.00 output per 1M tokens for Gemini 4 Argon, rising to $4.00 and $20.00 on 1 December 2026. There is no free tier.

Model prices decide what a team of agents costs to run. [Munder Difflin](https://harnessmd.com/download) is free and open source: a desktop app that runs a team of coding agents such as Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI on your own computer. The [install guide](/blog/how-to-install-and-use-munder-difflin/) covers setup, the [Guides hub](/blog/topics/guides/) has more price pages, and our [Gemini 4 Argon page](/blog/gemini-4-argon/) covers the launch.

## What does Gemini 4 Argon cost through the API?

Gemini 4 Argon costs $2.00 input and $10.00 output per 1M tokens through November 30, 2026, on Google's [pricing page](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing) as read on 13 Oct 2026. The output price includes thinking tokens, so a model that thinks for a long time bills for it.

| Paid tier, per 1M tokens | Through 30 Nov 2026 | From 1 Dec 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Input | $2.00 | $4.00 |
| Output, including thinking tokens | $10.00 | $20.00 |
| Context caching | $0.10 | $0.20 |
| Batch input | $1.00 | $2.00 |
| Batch output | $5.00 | $10.00 |

Caching is automatic only. The pricing page says "Implicit caching only (explicit caching is not supported)".

## When does the launch price end?

The launch price ends on 30 November 2026. Google's [launch post](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-argon/) says "After the introductory period expires on November 30, 2026, the price of $4 per 1M input tokens and $20 per 1M output tokens will apply."

Every figure doubles on the same day. An introductory price is a free sample with a calendar attached.

## Is there a free tier?

No. Google's pricing page marks each Free Tier cell for Gemini 4 Argon "Not available", and adds that the model "is not available on the Free tier or on the Google AI Pro plan".

Subscriptions are a separate route. Google's [plans page](https://one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans/) said on 13 Oct 2026 that the model is "Available to Google AI Ultra and rolling out to Google AI Pro subscribers in select countries". Check that page for the plan price in your country.

## What would a real job cost?

A job that reads 500,000 tokens and writes 50,000 costs $1.50 at the launch price and $3.00 from 1 December 2026. This is our arithmetic on Google's listed prices, with thinking tokens counted inside the output figure.

| Job | Through 30 Nov 2026 | From 1 Dec 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 500,000 tokens in, 50,000 out | $1.50 | $3.00 |
| 900,000 tokens in, 100,000 out | $2.80 | $5.60 |
| The same two jobs by batch | $0.75 and $1.40 | $1.50 and $2.80 |

One agent run is cheap. A team of agents running all night is the same sum with more zeros.

## How does it compare with Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra?

Gemini 4 Argon is the cheapest of the three on list price through 30 November 2026. From 1 December 2026 it costs exactly what Anthropic lists for Claude Opus 5.5 and what Meta lists for Muse Wonder. Three vendors, one price tag. All four price pages were read on 13 Oct 2026.

| Per 1M tokens | Input | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gemini 4 Argon, through 30 Nov 2026 | $2.00 | $10.00 |
| Gemini 4 Argon, from 1 Dec 2026 | $4.00 | $20.00 |
| Muse Wonder | $4.00 | $20.00 |
| Claude Opus 5.5 | $4 | $20 |
| GPT-6 Astra, short context | $10.00 | $50.00 |

The other rows come from Meta's model page, as the [Muse Wonder page](/blog/muse-wonder/) records, Anthropic's [pricing page](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing) and OpenAI's [pricing page](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing). On 13 Oct 2026 OpenAI also listed a higher long context rate of $20.00 and $75.00.

## What else is on the bill?

Search grounding is the main extra. Google's pricing page lists "5,000 free search requests per month (shared across all Gemini 3 and newer models), then $14 per 1,000 requests".

Antigravity has its own page. Google's [Antigravity pricing page](https://antigravity.google/pricing) lists an individual plan at "$0/month" and, for eligible Gemini Enterprise customers, "Standard/Plus plans starting at $30 USD per seat per month". Google's [Antigravity post](https://antigravity.google/blog/gemini-4-argon-in-google-antigravity) puts Gemini 4 Argon there for Google AI Ultra subscribers.

## How should you budget for it?

Budget at $4.00 and $20.00, and treat anything you save before December as a bonus. Run long jobs by batch where a delay is fine, since batch is half price. For other tools, see [Codex pricing](/blog/codex-pricing/) and [how much Claude Code costs](/blog/how-much-does-claude-code-cost/).
