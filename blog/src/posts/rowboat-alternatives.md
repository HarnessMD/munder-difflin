## What is Rowboat?

Rowboat is a free, open source set of apps for working with AI agents, made by Rowboat Labs. Its [README](https://github.com/rowboatlabs/rowboat), read 13 Nov 2026, leads with "Your agent team, in one group chat." Its [Y Combinator page](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/rowboat-labs) calls it an "Open-source AI coworker with memory" that "builds a local knowledge graph from your work". The licence is Apache 2.0, [v1.0.9](https://github.com/rowboatlabs/rowboat/releases/tag/v1.0.9) came out on 11 Nov 2026, and the repo showed 18,188 stars on 13 Nov 2026.

## How does Rowboat run?

It runs on the web, on iPhone, and as a desktop app for Mac, Windows and Linux, per the README. The desktop app "is a personal AI assistant that runs on your machine", with an email client, a meeting note taker and a code mode for Claude Code or Codex. It connects to Gmail, Calendar and Drive, and to other tools through MCP servers or Composio.

## What are Rowboat's knowledge graph and Spaces?

The knowledge graph is the desktop app's memory, and Spaces are shared chats for people and agents. The README says Rowboat "indexes email, meetings, slack and assistant conversations" into a backlinked graph, and "everything lives on your machine as plain Markdown".

## How much does Rowboat cost?

Rowboat is free, per its README read 13 Nov 2026: "Free. Bring your own agents or spin up hosted ones."

## Why look for a Rowboat alternative?

Our view: for stability, a simpler setup, or a plan you already pay for. The README says parts of the Spaces protocol are "deliberately still v0" and adds "Expect breaking changes while we dogfood." Optional voice and Exa search need your own Deepgram, ElevenLabs and Exa keys. Otherwise, this list is for people comparing tools like Rowboat.

## 1. AnythingLLM: the closest match to Rowboat's memory

AnythingLLM is a free, open source AI app that learns from your files on your computer. Its [home page](https://anythingllm.com), read 13 Nov 2026, says "A private AI assistant that runs entirely on your computer." The [README](https://github.com/Mintplex-Labs/anything-llm) lists "Automatic & User Managed Memories", and [v1.19.0](https://github.com/Mintplex-Labs/anything-llm/releases/tag/v1.19.0) came out on 26 Oct 2026. [Cloud](https://anythingllm.com/cloud) plans started at $50 a month on 13 Nov 2026. We think it suits people who want local memory without a group chat.

## 2. Munder Difflin: a team of agents on your own computer

We build this one. Its [README](https://github.com/HarnessMD/munder-difflin), read 13 Nov 2026, says "each agent gets long-term memory, a mailbox, and a desk on a 2D office floor". Work arrives from Slack or a webhook, and agents gain tools through MCP servers, skills and plugins. Agents run on your computer, or in our Cloud Office, always on, with your computer off; a mobile app is coming soon. We think it suits several agents sharing research, writing, ops and code. Setup is in [how to install and use Munder Difflin](/blog/how-to-install-and-use-munder-difflin/).

## 3. Eigent: an agent workforce on your desktop

Eigent is an open source Cowork desktop for [Mac, Windows and Linux](https://www.eigent.ai/download) where several agents split one job. Its [README](https://github.com/eigent-ai/eigent), read 13 Nov 2026, lists a "Multi-Agent Workforce" under Apache 2.0, and [v1.0.7](https://github.com/eigent-ai/eigent/releases/tag/v1.0.7) came out on 30 Oct 2026. The [pricing page](https://www.eigent.ai/pricing) listed a Free plan with your own keys, and Plus at $19.99 a month, on 13 Nov 2026. We think it suits people who want agents to divide one big task.

## 4. AionUi: one window for many agent CLIs

AionUi is a free desktop app where AI agents work on your computer. Its [README](https://github.com/iOfficeAI/AionUi), read 13 Nov 2026, calls it "a Cowork platform where AI agents work alongside you on your computer", under Apache 2.0. Its WebUI opens it from a phone. [Commits](https://github.com/iOfficeAI/AionUi/commits/main) landed through 5 Nov 2026. We think it suits people who already run Claude Code or Codex; see [AionUi alternatives](/blog/aionui-alternatives/).

## 5. Claude Cowork: if you already pay for Claude

Cowork hands Claude complex tasks to finish on your behalf. Anthropic's [help page](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork), read 13 Nov 2026, says it "runs your sessions remotely in the cloud" and that projects keep "their own files, links, instructions, and memory". It needs a paid plan, and [Claude's pricing page](https://claude.com/pricing) listed Pro at $20 if billed monthly on 13 Nov 2026. We think it suits people who already pay for Claude; see [Claude Cowork alternatives](/blog/claude-cowork-alternatives/).

## How to choose

Pick by what it should remember and who works in it. Our view:

- AnythingLLM for one private assistant that learns your documents.
- Munder Difflin for several agents with their own memory, doing research, writing, ops and code.
- Eigent for agents splitting one large task.
- AionUi if you already run several agent CLIs.
- Claude Cowork if you already pay for Claude.
- Rowboat for a knowledge graph of your email and a shared agent chat. It is the only pick with oars.
