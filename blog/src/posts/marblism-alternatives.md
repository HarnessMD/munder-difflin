## What is Marblism?

Marblism sells AI employees to businesses, each with a name and one job. Its [Y Combinator page](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/marblism), read 12 Nov 2026, calls them "AI Employees who love overtime" and lists the company in the Winter 2024 batch. The [pricing page](https://www.marblism.com/pricing) names the cast: "Your AI team can manage your inbox (Eva), run your social media (Sonny), generate leads (Stan), write SEO content (Penny), answer the phone (Rachel), build your website (Walter), and review contracts (Linda). All 7 AI Employees are included with every plan."

## How does Marblism work?

You use it on the web and in its mobile apps, and the employees sign in to your tools. The [home page](https://www.marblism.com) says "Your AI employees log into your inbox, calendar, socials and website, and work where you work." Its [What's new page](https://www.marblism.com/news) added a web browser on 12 Oct 2026 and Rooms on 4 Nov 2026, where several employees and your teammates "work on the same project together".

## How much does Marblism cost?

One plan has every employee, at $24 a month on the yearly option, per the pricing page read 12 Nov 2026; the monthly toggle showed $44 a month that day. Every plan "starts with 50 monthly hours of AI work that reset each month", and when the hours run out you buy top ups. The table also rates "Office drama" and "Sick days" as "none", which no paper company in Scranton could promise.

## Why look for a Marblism alternative?

Our view: for the hours, the yearly billing, or a plan you already pay for. Advanced work draws on 50 hours a month, the $24 price is billed yearly per a note on the pricing page read 12 Nov 2026, and paid Claude plans and xAI's Premium plan already include an agent. Marblism's own AI Employee Index, on the same page, grades nine products on 27 jobs and ranks Lindy, Sintra and Grok Bot behind itself. This list is for people comparing tools like Marblism before they pick.

## 1. Sintra: the closest match to Marblism

Sintra sells 12 named AI employees, the nearest match to Marblism's model. Its [home page](https://sintra.ai), read 12 Nov 2026, lists helpers such as Soshie for social media, Seomi for SEO and Vizzy as a virtual assistant, "Available 24/7 on web and mobile". Its [pricing page](https://sintra.ai/pricing) showed sale prices of $15.60 a month yearly and $48.50 monthly on 12 Nov 2026. It says it "won't work" if you want "A power tool with AI coding ability". It suits solo owners who want a named cast.

## 2. Munder Difflin: free, open source, your own computer

Munder Difflin is ours. Its [README](https://github.com/HarnessMD/munder-difflin), read 12 Nov 2026, says "Import a ready made role from the Agent Gallery if you would rather not start from scratch." and offers "Download 0.5.6 for macOS, Windows or Linux". Work arrives from Slack or a webhook, and agents gain tools through MCP servers, skills and plugins. Agents run on your computer, or in its Cloud Office, always on, with your computer off; a mobile app is coming soon. Our view: it suits research, writing, ops and code. Setup is in [how to install and use Munder Difflin](/blog/how-to-install-and-use-munder-difflin/).

## 3. Lindy: one assistant that also takes calls

Lindy is one AI employee that covers many roles. Its [home page](https://www.lindy.ai), read 12 Nov 2026, says "I work in Slack, email, text and your calendar, with 3,000+ integrations + MCP". Its [changelog](https://www.lindy.ai/changelog) added a phone number on 14 Oct 2026: "It calls businesses for you and answers as your receptionist." On 12 Nov 2026 the [pricing page](https://www.lindy.ai/pricing) showed paid use from $29.99 per user a month on some loads and $49.99 on others, after $50 in free credits. It suits teams that work in Slack.

## 4. Grok Bot: several Bots with set jobs

Grok Bot is xAI's set of AI teammates. The [Grok Bot page](https://x.ai/bot), read 12 Nov 2026, says "Every Bot has its own computer" and "Give each Bot its own job, and add another when the work grows." Version 1.0, per xAI's [launch post](https://x.ai/news/grok-bot-1-0) of 2 Nov 2026, works in Slack, Microsoft Teams and video calls. Premium was $30 a month billed monthly on 12 Nov 2026. It suits teams already on Premium or higher; see [Grok Bot alternatives](/blog/grok-bot-alternatives/).

## 5. Claude Cowork: if you already pay for Claude

Cowork hands Claude longer jobs. Anthropic's [help page](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork), read 12 Nov 2026, says it "brings Claude Code's agentic capabilities to knowledge work beyond coding" and "runs your sessions remotely in the cloud", on paid plans only. [Claude's pricing page](https://claude.com/pricing) listed Pro at $20 a month billed monthly on 12 Nov 2026. It suits people who already pay for Claude; see [Claude Cowork alternatives](/blog/claude-cowork-alternatives/).

## How to choose

Match the tool to how you already work. Our view:

- Sintra for a named cast on one plan, like Marblism.
- Munder Difflin for a free, open source team of agents for research, writing, ops and code.
- Lindy for one assistant across Slack, email and the phone.
- Grok Bot if you already pay for xAI's Premium plan or higher.
- Claude Cowork if you already pay for Claude.
- Stay on Marblism for a receptionist, a website builder and contract review in one plan.
