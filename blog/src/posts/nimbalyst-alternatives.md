Our view: Orca is the closest Nimbalyst alternative, because it is a free, MIT licensed desktop app that runs Claude Code and Codex side by side, each in its own worktree, with your GitHub and Linear tasks in one place. Checked 14 Nov 2026.

We make [Munder Difflin](https://harnessmd.com/download), which is free and open source: a desktop app that runs a team of AI agents for all kinds of knowledge work, coding included, on your own computer. See the [Comparisons hub](/blog/topics/comparisons/) and our pinned [Herdr alternatives](/blog/herdr-alternatives/) guide.

## What is Nimbalyst?

Nimbalyst is a desktop workspace that wraps Codex and Claude Code sessions with visual editors and task tracking. Its [README](https://github.com/Nimbalyst/nimbalyst), read 14 Nov 2026, calls it an "Open-source visual workspace for building with Codex, Claude Code, OpenCode, and other coding agents." The [Crystal README](https://github.com/stravu/crystal) says Crystal "has been deprecated and replaced by Nimbalyst".

## How does Nimbalyst run?

It runs on your computer and drives agents you already pay for. The README lists macOS, Windows and Linux, "with an iOS companion app". It runs "Codex and Claude Code side by side, plus OpenCode and GitHub Copilot in alpha and a Gemini provider." The [pricing page](https://nimbalyst.com/pricing/) says "You bring your own AI access".

## What do its session kanban and editors do?

The session kanban puts every agent session on one board, sorted by phase. The [home page](https://nimbalyst.com/) lists backlog, planning, implementing and complete. Its editors cover markdown, mockups, Mermaid, Excalidraw, CSV, data models and code, and the README says you "accept or reject each one" of the agent's proposed edits.

## How much does Nimbalyst cost, and is it open source?

Individual is $0 and Teams is $20 per user per month, per the [pricing page](https://nimbalyst.com/pricing/) read 14 Nov 2026, which adds "Teams is in Beta right now and free to use while it is". The home page says "The desktop and iOS apps are MIT licensed." The repo showed 2,070 stars on 14 Nov 2026, and [v0.83.0](https://github.com/nimbalyst/nimbalyst/releases/tag/v0.83.0) came out on 10 Nov 2026.

## Why look for a Nimbalyst alternative?

Our view: for wider agent support or a team price that holds after the beta. Its home page says "We are all managers of agents", and its [docs](https://nimbalyst.com/docs/developer-features/agent-teams-and-super-loops/) mark Agent Teams, where one session can "spawn parallel teammate agents", as experimental. Otherwise, this list is for people comparing tools like Nimbalyst before they pick.

## 1. Orca: the closest match to Nimbalyst

Orca runs Claude Code, Codex and other agents in parallel, each in its own worktree. Its [README](https://github.com/stablyai/orca), read 14 Nov 2026, says "See your GitHub and Linear tasks in one place, and start a workspace for each when you're ready to build." Hand one agent a big task and "It splits the work, starts worker agents in their own workspaces, and brings their results back to you." Its [home page](https://orca.dev/) says "Free and open source." [v1.4.258](https://github.com/stablyai/orca/releases/tag/v1.4.258) came out on 13 Nov 2026. We think it suits developers who want a lead agent; see [Orca vs Munder Difflin](/blog/orca-vs-munder-difflin/).

## 2. Munder Difflin: a team of AI agents for all kinds of knowledge work

Munder Difflin runs a team of AI agents for all kinds of knowledge work, coding included. Its [README](https://github.com/HarnessMD/munder-difflin), read 14 Nov 2026, describes "A GOD orchestrator you talk to. It routes tasks, adjudicates traffic, and escalates only what needs a human." Its Command Center has "Kanban tasks with dependencies". Send it work from Slack or a webhook, and give agents MCP servers, skills and plugins. The Cloud Office runs your agents in the cloud, always on, so work goes on with your computer off. We think it suits anyone who wants several agents on research, writing, ops and code at once; setup is in [how to install and use Munder Difflin](/blog/how-to-install-and-use-munder-difflin/).

## 3. T3 Code: steer agents from your phone

T3 Code drives the agents already on your machine from one interface. Its [README](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code), read 14 Nov 2026, says "Start a thread at your desk, then approve the next step from your phone." It works with Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenCode and more, with iOS and Android apps. Its [home page](https://t3.codes/) says "T3 Code is free, open source and fast." [v0.1.9](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code/releases/tag/v0.1.9) came out on 11 Nov 2026. We think it suits people who want threads more than boards; see [T3 Code alternatives](/blog/t3-code-alternatives/).

## 4. Paradev: worktrees and Linear on a Mac

Paradev, formerly Superset, is a desktop app for running CLI coding agents in parallel. Its [README](https://github.com/ParadevAI/Paradev), read 14 Nov 2026, says "Each task gets its own Git worktree, branch, and terminals" and "Windows is not yet available." The [pricing page](https://paradev.com/pricing) listed Free at $0 and Pro at $20 per user a month on 14 Nov 2026, with Linear and Slack in Pro. [Desktop v2.0.8](https://github.com/ParadevAI/Paradev/releases/tag/desktop-v2.0.8) came out on 10 Nov 2026. We think it suits Mac users who start work from Linear; see [Paradev alternatives](/blog/paradev-alternatives/).

## 5. Emdash Classic: open source, now on fixes

Emdash Classic is an Apache 2.0 desktop app where each task runs in its own Git worktree. Its [README](https://github.com/generalaction/emdash), read 14 Nov 2026, says "Bring the CLI agents you already use: Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Amp, and more." The [Classic page](https://emdash.com/classic) says it "keeps getting fixes, but new development has moved to Emdash 2.0". [v1.2.11](https://github.com/generalaction/emdash/releases/tag/v1.2.11) came out on 6 Nov 2026. We think it suits people who want an open source app on any OS; see [Emdash alternatives](/blog/emdash-alternatives/).

## How to choose

Pick by how you work with agents. Our view:

- Orca for parallel sessions with a lead agent.
- Munder Difflin for a team of AI agents for all kinds of knowledge work, on your computer or in the Cloud Office.
- T3 Code to steer agents from your phone.
- Paradev on a Mac, with tasks from Linear.
- Emdash Classic for Apache 2.0 on any OS.
- Nimbalyst itself for editors beside your sessions. No shame in staying put.
