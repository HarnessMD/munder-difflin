## Top launch

1. **Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2.** Anthropic's page, dated 9 Nov, says the two "are the same underlying model", and that Fable "has strong cybersecurity and biology safeguards to guard against misuse, which allows us to make it available to all users." They "generate outputs up to 30% faster than Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1", the vendor's own figures, at "$8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens" as of 9 Nov. 431 points and 732 comments on [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50429068) by 10 Nov. [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2)

## Top 2 skills, MCP servers and plugins

## Top 5 moves from multi agent tools

1. **Claude Code 2.1.321.** Published 9 Nov at 19:22 UTC. It adds Claude Fable 5.2, "now the default Fable model", and fixes "MCP OAuth requests sending secrets such as refresh tokens to another host when a server redirected them" and "a case where a read-only Bash command ran outside the sandbox without a permission prompt". Version 2.1.322, at 02:29 UTC on 10 Nov, adds "fast mode on Vertex in the global, us and eu regions". See [Claude Code privacy and secrets](/blog/claude-code-privacy-and-secrets/). [Source](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/releases/tag/v2.1.321)
2. **Orca 1.4.255.** Published 9 Nov at 12:24 UTC. "A new Agents tab lists agents across your connected desktops." Also: "Switching Codex accounts moves your open Codex chats to the account you choose." Git Graph, new a day earlier, now lets you "Check out, cherry-pick or revert commits". [Source](https://github.com/stablyai/orca/releases/tag/v1.4.255)
3. **T3 Code v0.1.8.** A stable release, published 9 Nov at 22:46 UTC. The notes list "agents ask for secrets through request_secret instead of chat", "render MCP Apps from Claude tool calls" and "list Claude models reported by Claude Code". See [T3 Code alternatives](/blog/t3-code-alternatives/). [Source](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code/releases/tag/v0.1.8)
4. **Pi v1.5.1.** Published 9 Nov at 23:47 UTC. You can now "use `pi auth login <provider>` and `pi auth logout <provider>` without starting a session", and "eligible caches now stay warm for up to 30 minutes between runs when a refresh is expected to pay for itself." The `anthropic` provider's default model "is now Claude Opus 5.5". See [Pi agent alternatives](/blog/pi-agent-alternatives/). [Source](https://github.com/earendil-works/pi/releases/tag/v1.5.1)
5. **Goose 1.58.0.** Published 9 Nov at 19:07 UTC. New features include a "Context window report and viewer", a "Lemonade provider" and "Collapse thinking by default with a setting to control it (desktop)". One fix: "Use recipe settings in scheduled runs". [Source](https://github.com/aaif-goose/goose/releases/tag/v1.58.0)

## Top 2 rising repos

1. **Astryx.** Meta's "open source design system that's fully customizable and agent ready". The README says it "grew inside Meta over the last eight years" and is "Currently in Beta". 3,278 stars in a day on GitHub trending on 10 Nov, up from 96 in a day on 8 Nov, 20,792 in total. MIT. [Source](https://github.com/meta/astryx)

MIT, created 8 Nov at 21:30 UTC and missed by the 9 Nov edition; 111 stars on 10 Nov. See [herdr alternatives](/blog/herdr-alternatives/). [Source](https://github.com/stevenmichaelthomas/numbrone)

## Top 5 things people are talking about

1. **Is Opus 5.5 nerfed?** Some users say so. The Hacker News item "Is Opus 5.5 nerfed? Thinking patterns changed on November 3" had 778 points and 484 comments by 10 Nov, posted 9 Nov at 07:04 UTC. This rests on the title and the thread. Anthropic's 9 Nov page still calls Opus 5.5 its "strongest daily driver". [Source](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50422069)
2. **Does ChatGPT Desktop forward your SSH agent?** The poster says it does: "The agent running on a remote machine can authenticate using your local SSH credentials. That means it can push to your private repos on GitHub, for example." The post points to openai/codex pull request 28131, titled "[codex] Refresh SSH agent for app-server proxy". 207 points by 10 Nov, posted 9 Nov at 08:17 UTC. [Source](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50422628)
3. **Does Gemini send Drive files to human reviewers?** A GitHub repo created 9 Nov claims so, under the title "Google Gemini routes personal Drive files and emails to human annotators". It says the capture is "probabilistic". These are the author's claims. 523 points on [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50429426) by 10 Nov. [Source](https://github.com/thisisme6598/gemini_files)
4. **Can Fable 5.2 build a whole game?** Lex Toumbourou says it "one-shotted my nephew's dream game from a bunch of drawings". His first version, shipped in June, was built "mostly with Claude Code, using Opus 4.8 and Fable 5". 306 points and 220 comments on [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50433659) by 10 Nov. [Source](https://notesbylex.com/fable-5-2-one-shotted-my-nephews-dream-game)
5. **Is Fable 5.2 the top model on Artificial Analysis?** The Hacker News title says "highest so far". The Artificial Analysis page says Fable 5.2 "scores 65 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index" against a median of 40, and calls it "notably slow" at 46.4 minutes per task. Smart and slow: a familiar office type. 329 points and 239 comments on [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50431144) by 10 Nov. [Source](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/claude-fable-5-2)
