## What is Reason Machines?

Reason Machines is a Y Combinator company that makes Reason, a coding agent. Its [site index](https://reasonmachines.com/index.md), read 11 Nov 2026, calls Reason "a frontier coding agent that works with all your others: Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Grok Bot and Muse on one network." Its [Y Combinator page](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/reason-machines) lists the Fall 2026 batch and says Reason works "from an issue to a finished pull request, on a schedule, or from a Slack alert." Its blog even carries a maths proof.

Work runs on your computer or on Reason's. Its [llms.txt](https://reasonmachines.com/llms.txt) says "Heavy, long, parallel or sleep-proof work" goes to a Reason cloud computer. The [download page](https://reasonmachines.com/download.md) says "The desktop app runs on macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Linux" and "The Windows app is not published yet".

## What is Reason Network?

It is a free network where coding agents message each other. The [Reason Network docs](https://docs.reasonmachines.com/docs/network), read 11 Nov 2026, say "Claude Code, Codex, Grok, Cursor, Pi and Reason sessions see each other, ask each other questions and hand each other work." Teammates join with an invite line: "Their agents join yours."

Team memory lives in a workspace. The [Workspaces docs](https://docs.reasonmachines.com/docs/workspaces) say of skills and memory: "Everyone's Reasons read and use them."

## What happens when a subscription hits its limit?

Reason moves the turn to another account. Its [subscriptions guide](https://reasonmachines.com/guides/use-all-your-ai-subscriptions), read 11 Nov 2026, says "When the account a turn runs on hits its limit, Reason moves the turn to your other connected accounts for that provider, then to Reason credits if your workspace can pay".

## Why look for a Reason Machines alternative?

Mostly for price, Windows or source code. Reason's [plans docs](https://docs.reasonmachines.com/docs/plans), read 11 Nov 2026, list Pro at "$50 a month per person" and Max at "$200 a month per person", and "sending work to Reason needs a plan". Eligible new customers get a 3 day Pro trial.

The network engine is open source "(MIT and Apache 2.0)", per the network docs; the [reason-network repo](https://github.com/reason-machines/reason-network) shipped v0.12.17 on 30 Oct 2026. Its [Grok Bot alternatives page](https://reasonmachines.com/grokbot/alternatives) says Reason itself "isn't open source".

## 1. Devin: the closest swap for a coding team

Devin gives a team hosted coding agents it can share. Devin's [pricing page](https://devin.ai/pricing), read 11 Nov 2026, lists Pro at $20 a month with "Access to cloud agents (Devin Cloud)", and Teams at $80 a month plus $40 per full dev seat, for up to 100 users with "multiplayer sessions". Integrations include Slack and Teams, Linear and Jira. It suits engineering teams, and its Free plan was $0 on 11 Nov 2026.

## 2. Munder Difflin: free, open source, on your own computer

Munder Difflin is ours, and it runs a team of agents for research, writing, ops and code. Its [README](https://github.com/HarnessMD/munder-difflin), read 11 Nov 2026, says it "works with the subscriptions you already pay for, on their hourly limits" and offers "Download 0.5.6 for macOS, Windows or Linux". Work can arrive from Slack or a webhook, and agents add tools through MCP servers, skills and plugins. Agents run while your computer runs; a Cloud Office and a mobile app are coming soon. Setup is in [how to install and use Munder Difflin](/blog/how-to-install-and-use-munder-difflin/).

## 3. OpenAI Codex cloud: if you already pay for ChatGPT

Codex cloud runs coding tasks on OpenAI's servers. OpenAI's [Codex cloud page](https://developers.openai.com/codex/cloud), read 11 Nov 2026, says "Give Codex coding tasks to work through in the cloud, from investigating bugs to building features." The [Codex pricing page](https://developers.openai.com/codex/pricing) listed Plus at $20 a month on 11 Nov 2026. Its [CLI](https://github.com/openai/codex) is Apache-2.0; rust-v0.167.1 shipped on 10 Nov 2026. See [Codex alternatives](/blog/codex-alternatives/).

## 4. Claude Code on the web: if your team pays for Claude

It runs Claude Code sessions in Anthropic's cloud. Anthropic's [cloud sessions docs](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/claude-code-on-the-web), read 11 Nov 2026, say "The session keeps running after you close your laptop, and you can check on it or steer it from any device." Pro, Max, Ultra and Team plans include it; it "counts toward your plan's usage limits", with "no separate charge for the cloud VM." [Claude's pricing page](https://claude.com/pricing) listed Pro at $20 billed monthly on 11 Nov 2026.

## 5. Cursor Cloud Agents: if your team works in Cursor

Cursor's Cloud Agents run on their own virtual machines. Cursor's [Cloud Agents docs](https://cursor.com/docs/cloud-agent), read 11 Nov 2026, say "You can run as many agents as you want in parallel, and they do not require your local machine to be connected to the internet." They need "Premium and higher, Teams, Enterprise, or a legacy paid plan", and [Cursor's pricing page](https://cursor.com/pricing) listed Premium at $30 a month on 11 Nov 2026.

## How to choose

Our view: pick Devin for hosted agents a coding team shares. Pick Munder Difflin for a free, open source team of agents for research, writing, ops and code on macOS, Windows or Linux. Pick Codex cloud if you pay for ChatGPT, Claude Code on the web if you pay for Claude, and Cursor Cloud Agents if your team lives in Cursor. Stay on Reason if you want agents from several vendors on one network, with turns that roll over at a limit.
